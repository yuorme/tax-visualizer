HEATING ASSISTANCE REBATE 2026-2027
Application Form – Apply by March 31, 2027
Please complete the application below. You may be eligible for the rebate if:
• you are responsible for your home heating costs, and
• you meet the household income requirements in Section 3, and
• you date and sign the back of the application.

To help us process your application faster, we strongly recommend that you include a heating bill if:
• this is your first time applying for the Heating Assistance Rebate, or
• you have moved since you last received the rebate
• you live in a multi-unit dwelling
If you do not include a bill, we may request proof of heat at a later time.
 PICK YOUR HOUSEHOLD TYPE AND PROVE YOUR INCOME. (Select one)
❑ Receiving Income Assistance from the Department of Opportunities and Social Development.
The applicant must be the recipient; no proof of income is required. Provide signature in Section 4.
❑ Single, with a 2025 net income of $30,000 or less.
You live alone, with no children or dependents. Provide signature in Section 4.
❑ Single-parent household, with a 2025 net income of $45,000 or less.
You are the sole adult residing with children aged 17 or younger, and you must be receiving the Canada
Child Benefit. Provide signature in Section 4.
❑ Household of 2 or more adults, with a combined 2025 net income of $45,000 or less.
You reside with a spouse or common-law partner, children 18 or older, roommates or other adults. Provide
signatures in Section 4. Any additional adults living in the household and not already listed on this
application must provide a 2025 Notice of Assessment from the Canada Revenue Agency.
❑ Receiving Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS) or The Allowance.
You are currently receiving the GIS or Allowance; no proof is required. Provide signature(s) in Section 4.
To learn more about eligibility for the GIS or Allowance, call Service Canada at 1-800-277-9914.

 WAIT TO RECEIVE YOUR REBATE.
If you currently receive your personal income tax refund from the Canada Revenue Agency by direct deposit, your rebate
will be deposited into the same bank account you have registered with CRA once it’s approved.
If you do not receive your income tax refund by direct deposit, your cheque will be mailed within 8 weeks.
If you provided your email address, we will email you if we require any additional information.
Keep an eye out for email notifications from HARP@novascotia.ca.
** Service Nova Scotia will never text message you with additional steps in order to receive your rebate **

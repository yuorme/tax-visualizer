# Social assistance payments – Personal income tax

- Federal: Line 14500
- Tax year: 2025| Prior tax years: 2024 and earlier

Social assistance payments are amounts paid to help with basic needs, such as food, clothing, and shelter. These payments can be made to you or to third parties, such as a landlord or mortgage lender. They are based on a means, needs, or income test.

If you received these payments, they are shown on Form T5007, Statement of Benefits.

These payments are not taxable. However, you must include them in your net income to ensure that any benefits and credits you may be entitled to are calculated correctly.

## Who needs to report

Reporting social assistance payments based on your situation note 1
Your situation | What to do
You are single | Report the amount shown on your T5007 slip.
You have a spouse or common-law partner and lived apart | Each person reports the amount shown on their own T5007 slip.
You have a spouse or common-law partner and lived together | The person with the higher net income reports all payments note 2 . If both amounts are the same, the person named on the T5007 slip reports the payments.
Notes Note 1 Certain social assistance payments received for being a foster parent or for caring for a disabled adult who lived with you do not have to be included. Return to note 1 referrer Note 2 If the payments are for caring for your spouse or common-law partner or an individual related to either of you, whoever has the higher net income will have to include those payments. Return to note 2 referrer

### How to determine who has the higher net income

To determine who has the higher net income:

- Each person calculates their net income (line 23600).
- Do not include in your net income:

- social assistance payments (line 14500)
- childcare expenses (line 21400)
- social benefits repayment (line 23500)

- Compare the results.

After comparing the results:

- The person with the higher amount reports all payments.
- If both amounts are the same, the person named on the T5007 slip reports the payments.

### If your marital status changed during the year

Social assistance payments received prior to marriage or common-law partnership should be included in the recipient's income, whereas those received after marriage or partnership should be reported in accordance to the rules set out above.

## How to report

### Report the amount on the federal tax return

- Determine who must report the income by referring to the Who needs to report section above.
- Enter on line 14500 the amount shown in box 11 of the T5007 slip or, if applicable, the federal part of the Quebec RL-5 slip.
- Claim a deduction for the same amount on line 25000.

Note:

This ensures the amount is included in your net income but is not taxable. It also ensures that any benefits and credits you may be entitled to are calculated correctly.

### Keep your supporting documents

Do not send any supporting documents when you file your tax return. Keep them in case the CRA asks to see them later.

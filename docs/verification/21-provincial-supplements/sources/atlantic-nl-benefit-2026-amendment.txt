NEWFOUNDLAND AND LABRADOR
REGULATION 17/26

Income Supplement and Seniors' Benefit Regulations (Amendment)
under the
Income Tax Act, 2000
(O.C. 2026-087)

(Filed May 1, 2026)

Under the authority of section 68 of the Income Tax Act, 2000, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following regulations.

Dated at St. John’s, May 1, 2026.

Karen Stone KC
Clerk of the Executive Council

REGULATIONS

Analysis

2. S.3.1 Amdt.

NLR 32/16
as amended

1. Paragraph 3(c) of the Income Supplement and Seniors' Benefit Regulations is amended by

(a) deleting the amount "$1,516" wherever it appears and substituting the amount $1,882"; and

(b) deleting the amount "$29,402" and substituting the amount "$30,409".

2. Section 3.1 of the regulations is amended by renumbering it as subsection 3.1(1) and adding immediately after subsection (1) the following:

(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), indexation required under that subsection shall not be carried out for the 2026 taxation year.

Commencement

3. These regulations come into force on July 1, 2026.

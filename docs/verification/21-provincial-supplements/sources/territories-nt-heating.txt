# Senior Home Heating Subsidy

See all Funding Programs

Dene Kǝdǝ́, Dëne Sųłıné, Dene Zhatıé, Dinjii Zhuʼ Ginjik, Inuinnaqtun, Inuktitut (syllabics), Inuktitut (roman orthography), Inuvialuktun, Tłı̨chǫ, nēhiyawēwin

## Overview

The Senior Home Heating Subsidy assists low-to-modest income seniors in the NWT with their heating costs. Remember to budget for your heating costs. Seniors are responsible for heating costs above the subsidy level.

## Available Funding

Eligible seniors will receive a monthly amount from September through April that is dependent on the community that they live in. Unused portions of the monthly allotments can be carried forward to the following month.

We recognize that navigating the application process can be challenging and encourage seniors to involve an advocate or family member in meetings, should they feel additional support is beneficial.

## Income Threshold

The income threshold is the maximum annual household income a senior can have to qualify for the subsidy. There is a single NWT-wide threshold of $90,000, so seniors in any zone with a household income of $90,000 or less are eligible to apply.

## Subsidies for 2026-2027

- Zone 1: $530 per month, up to $4,240
- Zone 2: $685 per month, up to $5,480
- Zone 3: $1,090 per month, up to $8,720

## Zones

- Zone 1 communities: Behchokǫ̀, Dettah, Enterprise, Fort Providence, Fort Simpson, Fort Smith, Hay River, K’atł’odeeche, Kakisa, Ndılǫ, and Yellowknife.
- Zone 2 communities: Fort Liard, Fort Resolution, Gametì, Inuvik, Jean Marie River, Nahanni Butte, Whatì, and Wrigley.
- Zone 3 communities: Aklavik, Colville Lake, Délı̨nę, Fort Good Hope, Fort McPherson, Łutselk’e, Norman Wells, Paulatuk, Sachs Harbour, Saamba K'e, Tsiigehtchic, Tuktoyaktuk, Tulita, Ulukhaktok, and Wekweètì.

## Deadline

Applications for the 2026-2027 heating season (September through April) can be submitted anytime up to March 15, 2027.

## Eligibility

This program is intended for Northerners who:

- Are residents of the Northwest Territories
- Are 60 years of age or older
- Own and occupy the home as their primary residence or, if renting, be the lessee of a self-contained unit where heating costs are not included in the rent
- Meet the household income threshold
- Are not receiving Income Assistance
- Apply each year

## How to Apply

To apply, a Senior Home Heating Subsidy Application must be completed during the application period noted above. Proof of age, total household income, homeownership or rental accommodation must be provided to a local ECE Service Centre.

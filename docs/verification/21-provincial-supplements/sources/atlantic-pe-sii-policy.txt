1.0 PURPOSE
1.1 To define the eligibility requirements, identify the funded services available, and to
provide funding for eligible services through the Seniors Independence Initiative (SII).
2.0 DEFINITIONS
2.1 Agent: an adult person who applies for, or has applied for, assistance on behalf of an
applicant.
2.2 Applicant: a person who has applied for, on whose behalf an application is made, or is in
receipt of funding through SII.
2.3 Co-Applicant: the spouse of an applicant and includes a person who, although not
married to the applicant, lives with the applicant as if they were married.
2.4 Income: the combined income of the applicant and co-applicant as defined by line
23600 on the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) income tax return, adjusted in accordance
with program requirements.
2.5 Permanent residency status: a status granting someone who is not a Canadian citizen
the right to live and work in Canada without any time limits on length of stay.
2.6 Primary Residence: a dwelling where an individual ordinarily resides and considers it to
be their principal home.
2.7 Resident of Prince Edward Island: a person who is legally entitled to remain in Canada
and maintains a primary residence on Prince Edward Island.
2.8 Roommate: two or more people co-habiting without any inherent duty or obligation of
care.
2.9 Senior: a person who is 65 years of age or older.

3.0 POLICY STATEMENTS
3.1 SII provides funding for practical services to support seniors to remain in their home and
community under the provision of social services.
3.2 Eligible services include:
• communication;
• grass cutting;
• home maintenance;
• housekeeping;
• medical alert system;
• snow removal; and
• transportation
3.3 SII is intended to supplement the existing natural supports that an applicant receives
and will not delay, prevent, replace, or serve as an alternative to existing natural
supports or available government and community services.
3.4 An applicant is eligible for SII if the applicant:
• is a resident of Prince Edward Island;
• is lawfully entitled to be in or to remain in Canada with a permanent residency
status;
• is 65 years of age or older at the time of application;
• lives independently or with a spouse;
• has a household income below the eligibility threshold for a single person or a
couple;
• has combined assets of less than $100,000, excluding primary residence, land the
primary residence is on, and primary vehicle; and
• owns or rents their primary residence.
3.5 Applicants will be required to complete an Application and Asset Declaration Form
confirming their asset level. Applicants must be made aware that, should the applicant’s
file be subject to a random audit, the applicant will be required to provide documents to
verify assets. Verification documents may include, bank statements, investment
statements, and property tax information, and other documents as determined to be
required to verify assets.

3.6 For the purposes of determining an applicant’s eligibility, applicants residing as
roommates who are recognized as single individuals by CRA for income tax purposes are
considered to be living independently provided there is no caregiving obligation
between them. This determination excludes arrangements where a duty of care would
reasonably be assumed, such as, but not limited to, an adult child residing with a senior
parent.
3.7 Applicants living in a community care facility, long-term care facility, or in an
arrangement where their care is provided are not considered to be living independently
and therefore are not eligible for funding.
3.8 The amount of SII funding an applicant may receive is based on their household income.
Funding may be provided up to the maximum benefit level corresponding to the
household income, as outlined in the Schedule of Seniors Independence Initiative
Benefits.
3.9 SII requires verification of eligibility upon application and at minimum once every 12
months or when a change in circumstances occurs.
3.10 Benefits paid through SII are paid directly to the applicant or their designated agent.
Payments for services or service providers are the responsibility of the applicant.
3.11 Applicants must be informed that personal information is collected under Section 31(c)
of Prince Edward Island’s Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act and is
used for the purpose of the administration of SII.

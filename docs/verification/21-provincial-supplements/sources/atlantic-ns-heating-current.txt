# Apply for a heating assistance rebate: Heating Assistance Rebate Program

The Heating Assistance Rebate Program helps low-income Nova Scotians with the cost of home heating. Rebates are $400 for each household. You can apply 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027.

Apply to the Heating Assistance Rebate Program for help with the cost of home heating.

## Rebate amount

You can receive $400 if you qualify for the Heating Assistance Rebate Program.

## Eligible home heating costs include:

- heating oil
- electric heat
- natural gas heat
- wood
- wood pellets
- coal
- other heating sources

Ineligible home heating costs include:

- heating that’s included in rent
- electricity usage that’s less than 5,000 kWh per year
- heating for investment or income properties
- heating for seasonal or recreation properties
- heating for commercial or business properties

Review detailed eligibility for home heating costs and heating bill requirements in the Home Heating Costs Factsheet.

## Seniors Care Grant

You can also apply to the Seniors Care Grant for help with the cost of home heating.

## Eligibility

To qualify for the rebate, you need to pay for your own home (residential) heat and meet 1 of the following criteria:

- have a 2025 net income of $30,000 or less and live alone with no kids or dependents
- have a combined 2025 net household income of $45,000 or less and live with kids, dependents or other adults (to qualify as a single parent, you need to receive the Canada Child Benefit)
- receive Income Assistance from the Department of Opportunities and Social Development
- receive the Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS) or the Allowance from Service Canada

If your personal or financial situation has significantly changed since you filed your income tax for 2025, you still may qualify. Contact the Department of Service Nova Scotia to find out if you qualify.

You need to apply by 31 March 2027.

## How to apply

- Apply online for the rebate.
- Check the application for details on all required supporting documents.
- Submit your completed application and any supporting documents.
- Check your application status online (if you apply online).

## How long it takes

It should take 8 weeks to get your rebate. It can take longer if more information is needed or if your application hasn’t been filled in correctly. If more information is required to process your rebate, we may contact you by mail or email.

If your income tax refund is direct deposited, your heating assistance rebate is too. If not, your rebate is mailed to you.

The Department of Service Nova Scotia will never text or email you for banking information or ask you to click a link to "deposit" your rebate.

## Cost

There is no cost to apply for the rebate.

## Before you start

Make sure you have:

- all adults on the application available to sign the form
- Social Insurance Number and date of birth of all adults in your home

You may need to provide additional supporting documents including:

- 2025 Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment for you and any other adults in your home
- Canada Child Benefit Form
- proof of change in marital status
- proof of death (Death Certificate) for your spouse or partner, if applicable

The Heating Assistance Rebate Program may ask you to provide a heating bill or receipt. If the program asks you to provide a heating bill or receipt, your application can’t be processed until the information is provided.

If you’d like to make sure your application isn’t delayed while waiting for heating bill or receipt, you can provide that information when you apply. The program recommends that you include a heating bill or receipt when applying if:

- this is your first time applying for the Heating Assistance Rebate
- you moved since you last received the Heating Assistance Rebate
- you live in a multiple-unit dwelling

Heating bills and receipts need to include:

- your name and home address
- date of the home heating purchase or delivery
- details of the home heating cost
- cost of the home heating
- name and address of the person or business providing the home heating

## Apply online

When you apply online you need an email address. The Heating Assistance Rebate Program emails you if it needs more information to process your rebate. The program also emails you to let you know when your rebate is about to be direct deposited.

Start now

Other ways to apply

You can use the Heating Assistance Rebate Application Form (PDF 362 kB) if you’re unable to apply online. Send your completed application (both sides of the application) and any supporting documents by mail (application needs to be postmarked no later than 31 March 2027). If you’d like to receive a paper application by mail, contact us.

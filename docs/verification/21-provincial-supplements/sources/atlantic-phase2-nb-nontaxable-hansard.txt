Madam Deputy Speaker, one of the actions that has
been implemented is a change to the Standard Family
Contribution Policy that is aimed at reducing the
financial burden to the individual residing at home
when their spouse resides in a long-term care facility.
This regulation change was approved in December of
last year. The policy to ensure that the spouse at home
retains, at minimum, 60% of the net family income
was implemented on January 1, 2024. That is a good
news story.

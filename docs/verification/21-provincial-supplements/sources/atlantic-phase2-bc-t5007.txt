T5007 Tax Slips – Information for Clients
Seniors
1. How is a T5007 amount calculated for seniors?
 All benefits issued by the Province of British Columbia in the 2025 calendar year
(January to December 2025) are included on the T5007 tax slip for that tax year.
Retroactive payments from the previous year, paid in 2025, are also reflected in
the T5007.
Bus Pass for Seniors
 The benefit value for this provincial bus pass is $52 per month, less the annual $45
administration fee (replacement fees are not deducted from the total benefit calculation).
Senior’s Supplement
 The benefit value for the Senior’s Supplement is a maximum of $99.30 per month for a
single person, and $220.50 per month for a couple.
2. I am a senior. How will the T5007 impact my tax return?
 The Canada Revenue Agency requires that the ministry issue a tax receipt for anyone
who receives more than $500 in provincial benefits including the Senior's Supplement
and the Bus Pass program.
 The income shown on the T5007 tax slip is not taxable but is used to calculate
entitlement to tax credits like the GST credit and Canada Child Benefit, if applicable.
 It will not affect eligibility for income support programs, such as Old Age Security (OAS)
or the Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS).
 The T5007 amount will not impact the amount you receive through the provincial Shelter
Aid for Elderly Renters (SAFER) program, or what you pay for provincially subsidized
housing.
General
3. What is a T5007 Tax Slip?
 The T5007 is a tax slip that will help you prepare your income tax return as it includes
the amount of provincial assistance issued to you in a tax year.
4. Why is a T5007 Tax Slip issued to me?
 The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) requires that everyone who receives more than
$500 in provincial assistance reports this income on Lines 14500 and 25000 of their
federal income tax return, including Senior’s Supplement and Bus Pass for Seniors
recipients.
5. Why did I get more than one T5007 Tax Slip?
 If you receive more than $500 in 2025 from: Income or Disability Assistance, Hardship
Assistance, Senior’s Supplement or Bus Pass for Seniors, you will receive a separate
T5007 tax slip for each program.

 If you receive ministry assistance for more than one child under the Child in the Home of
a Relative (CIHR) program, you will receive a T5007 tax slip in your name for each child.
 If you received assistance for yourself and under the CIHR program for a relative’s
child, you will receive one T5007 tax slip for your own assistance and a separate one
for the assistance you received for the child.
6. How does a T5007 amount affect my tax return and federal and provincial benefits?
 The amount shown on the T5007 tax slip is not taxable but is used to calculate
entitlement to tax credits like the Goods and Services Tax (GST) credit and Canada
Child Benefit, if applicable.
 Amounts on the T5007 tax slip will not affect eligibility for income support programs,
such as the Old Age Security (OAS) or the Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS).
 The T5007 amount will not impact the amount you receive through the provincial Shelter
Aid for Elderly Renters (SAFER) program or what you pay for provincially subsidized
housing.
7. Why did I not a get T5007 Tax Slip in the mail?
 If you received $500 or less from: Income Assistance, Disability Assistance, Hardship
Assistance, Senior’s Supplement or Bus Pass for Seniors in a tax year, no tax slip will
be issued to you, and you are not required to report this income on your tax return.
 If your assistance file was closed on or before December 31 and you have moved from
the last address on your file, your original T5007 tax slip was mailed to your old address.
 If you received more than $500 in assistance in the tax year but made a large repayment
to the ministry (or an agency, such as Service Canada, made the repayment on your
behalf), there may not be any assistance payments left for you to report on your income
tax return.
8. Where can I get help to do my taxes?
 Government of Canada’s Community Volunteer Income Tax Program—helps you file your
tax returns. Contact: 1-800-959-8281 or visit: www.canada.ca and search “Free Tax
Clinics".
 Disability Alliance BC (DABC) Tax Aid Programs—offer free tax preparation services for
ministry clients. Tax Aid programs host online workshops and webinars about income tax
filing throughout the year. For more information, please visit: taxaiddabc.org or contact:
Vancouver Island: Together Against Poverty Society (TAPS)
o Interior: Ki-Low-Na Friendship Society
o North: Active Support Against Poverty Society (ASAP)
o Metro-Vancouver & Fraser Valley: Disability Alliance BC (DABC)
 British Columbia Aboriginal Network on Disability Society (BCANDS)—helps Indigenous
people living with a disability who may be eligible for the Disability Tax Credit (DTC) or a
Registered Disability Savings Plan (RDSP). Contact: 1-888-815-5511 or by email at
dcm2@bcands.bc.ca

9. How does a repayment of Income Assistance, Disability Assistance, or Hardship
Assistance affect my T5007 amount?
 Any repayments made to SDPR in the tax year would have been deducted from the
amount reported on the T5007 tax slip. If you are concerned that a repayment was not
deducted from the amount on the T5007 tax slip, please call the T5007 office at
1-877-815-2363; option 7 to review the calculation.
 If you, or an agency like Service Canada acting on your behalf, made a large repayment
to SDPR in the tax year, it is possible that there are no longer any income assistance
payments left for you to report on your tax return. You should call the T5007 office if you
would like to confirm that this is the reason why you did not receive a T5007 tax slip.
10.When is the T5007 Tax Slip mailed?
 T5007 tax slips were mailed at the end of February 2026.
 T5007 tax slips for income assistance and disability assistance recipients are also
available to My Self Service (MySS) registered users as of the end of February 2026.
11.Where is the T5007 Tax Slip mailed to?
The T5007 tax slip was mailed to the address on your assistance, Senior’s Supplement or
Bus Pass for Seniors file as of December 31, 2025:
 If your file is open and you moved before December 31 your T5007 tax slip was sent
to your new address.
 If your file is open and you moved after December 31 your T5007 tax slip was sent to
your old address.
 If your file was closed and you moved after it closed, your T5007 tax slip was sent to
your old address. You can obtain a reprint of your T5007 tax slip by calling 1 877 815-
2363; option 7.
12.I didn’t receive a T5007 Tax Slip, or I lost it. What should I do?
 If you have My Self Serve (MySS) you can access your T5007 and the most recent 6
years of tax filing information.
 If you’re not registered with MySS,you can obtain the information you need to
complete your tax return by calling the T5007 information line at 1-877-815-2363,
option 7 and request to obtain your T5007 total over the phone.
 You do not need to attach an actual T5007 tax slip to your tax return.
13.I am a CIHR provider. How will a T5007 tax slip affect my tax return?
 The income shown on a T5007 tax slip is not taxable but may affect the amount of your
tax credits.
 You are required by the CRA to report this income. However, the CRA exempts this
income for some types of relatives. You can contact the CRA toll-free at 1-800-959-8281
to find out if you qualify for this exemption.
14.Can I obtain a detailed breakdown on how my T5007 amount is calculated?

upon request. You can contact us toll free at 1 866 866-0800 for the ministry to mail you

Filing Your Income Tax Return
Canada Revenue Agency requires that the Province of British Columbia provide a
T5007 tax slip to all persons who received more than $500 from BC Employment and
Assistance during the tax year. Child care providers do not receive a T5007.
The amount on the T5007 should be included on your income tax return.

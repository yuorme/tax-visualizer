# Apply for help with home repairs and accessibility adaptations: Housing Repair and Accessibility Program

The Housing Repair and Accessibility Program helps low- and moderate-income homeowners with the cost of critical home repairs. The program also provides funding for accessibility adaptations (upgrades) to help people with disabilities and seniors stay in their homes.

Apply to the Housing Repair and Accessibility Program for help with the cost of home repairs and accessibility adaptations (upgrades).

You need to use the funding for:

- critical repairs and non-critical repairs for structural, electrical, plumbing or heating that may be a risk to a home occupant
- accessibility adaptations (like wheelchair ramps, accessible bathrooms, accessible kitchens and other changes) that allow you to live independently in your principal residence

A principal residence is the home you own and live in.

## Funding streams

The Housing Repair and Accessibility Program has 2 different funding streams: housing repair and accessible housing. Applicants can apply for funding from either or both funding streams.

## Funding amount

The Housing Repair Stream may fund up to $18,000 of eligible repair costs. The funding provides $10,000 as a grant and $8,000 as a forgivable loan. To have the loan forgiven, you must meet the loan conditions. The applicant or other sources need to fund the remaining balance of the costs.

The Accessible Housing Stream may fund up to $18,000 of eligible accessibility adaptations. The funding provides $10,000 as a grant and $8,000 as a forgivable loan. To have the loan forgiven, you must meet the loan conditions. The applicant or other sources need to fund the remaining balance of the costs.

For larger repair projects or accessibility adaptation projects an additional $20,000 may be available as a repayable loan (you need to pay the loan back over time).

### Repayable loan

The repayable loan is a combined maximum available across both funding streams. If you apply for funding from both the Housing Repair Stream and Accessible Housing Stream, the repayable loan may only fund up to $20,000.

## Eligible repairs and adaptations

### Housing repair

Eligible critical repairs are repairs or issues in 1 or more of the 4 critical situation categories that represent a hazard to a property or occupants and may result in the occupants having to leave the home.

Critical situation categories include:

- structural
- electrical
- plumbing
- heating

Eligible non-critical repairs are repairs for structural, electrical, plumbing or heating hazards that may be a risk to a home occupant if not addressed within a reasonable timeframe.

### Accessible housing

Adaptations need to directly address the disability or permanent reduced ability of the household member. Adaptations also need to be permanent and follow health and safety standards.

Eligible critical adaptations include:

- exterior ramp
- widening an exterior door or lower threshold
- chair lift

Eligible primary adaptations include kitchen, bathroom and other adaptations that address the disability or permanent reduced ability of the household member.

## Eligible costs

Eligible costs include:

- materials
- labour
- adaptation assessments
- building permits
- legal fees
- related taxes

Costs are not eligible if you complete the work before you’re approved for the funding.

## Accessing the funding

Once your application is approved, the program sends you a Letter of Approval that outlines the conditions of the loan or grant. If you receive a forgivable or repayable loan, you need to sign an agreement that outlines the terms and conditions of the loan. The Department of Growth, Development and Defence Industries pays invoices directly to contractors (after you’re satisfied with the work and send the Completion of Work Form to the program).

## Inspections

Inspections conducted by the Department of Growth, Development and Defence Industries (or its representatives) confirm that the required repairs and accessibility adaptations are needed and were completed. Inspections don’t confirm compliance with any required building codes, standards or the construction methods and materials used.

## Changes to repairs and adaptations

The Department of Growth, Development and Defence Industries needs to review and approve any changes to repairs and accessibility adaptations that are outlined in your bid package. If changes to repairs and accessibility adaptations are not approved by the department, you don’t receive funding for the repairs.

## Eligibility

You can apply for funding if:

- you and your co-applicant (if applicable) own and live in the home where the repairs or adaptations will be applied
- you own and have lived in your home for at least 1 year
- you have an annual pre-tax (gross) household income that’s not more than the income limits in the Housing Repair and Accessibility Program Guide
- you don’t have the financial ability to pay for the repairs or adaptations on your own based on your pre-tax (gross) household income and investments (households with more than $25,000 in investment assets are not eligible)
- your property taxes are paid up to date or you have a payment plan in place with the local government for at least 3 months
- your property value is no more than $360,000 in Halifax Regional Municipality and the County of East Hants or $280,000 in other regions of Nova Scotia

For the Housing Repair Stream, you need to meet the following stream criteria:

- repairs are critical to you remaining safely in your home
- your home is at least 5 years old
- you haven’t received previous housing repair grants in the last 5 years, unless the repairs are critical to you remaining safely in your home
- you haven’t received previous housing repair loans in the last 15 years, unless the repairs are critical to you remaining safely in your home

For the Accessible Housing Stream, you need to meet the following stream criteria:

- you or someone in your household has a disability or a permanent loss of or reduced ability (like a loss of mobility) that requires adaptation, if applicable (if you apply for the Accessible Housing Stream)
- you haven’t received previous accessible housing grants in the last 5 years, unless the adaptations are critical to you remaining safely in your home
- you haven’t received previous accessible housing loans in the last 15 years, unless the adaptations are critical to you remaining safely in your home

Eligible properties include:

- single-family detached house
- townhouse (semi-detached) or row house
- condominium property (interiors only, not common spaces)
- manufactured (mobile) home where ownership of the unit is verified

You also need to meet any additional eligibility criteria (like homeowner eligibility, property (house) eligibility and repair and adaptation eligibility) outlined in the Housing Repair and Accessibility Program Guide.

## How to apply

- Review detailed eligibility criteria in the Housing Repair and Accessibility Program Guide.
- Complete the application form.
- Check the application for details on all required supporting documents.
- Submit your completed application and supporting documents.
- Department of Growth, Development and Defence Industries reviews your application and confirms your eligibility.
- An inspector from Department of Growth, Development and Defence Industries visits your home to confirm the required repairs.
- If the inspector confirms that the required repairs are needed and meet eligibility requirements, the Department of Growth, Development and Defence Industries sends you a bid package outlining the repairs you need to get quotes from contractors for.
- Send your quotes to the Department of Growth, Development and Defence Industries for review and approval.

## How long it takes

For emergency repairs (like no heat in winter or no water), it should take 1 to 3 days for the Department of Growth, Development and Defence Industries to review your funding application and let you know if your funding application is approved. It can take longer if more information is needed or if your application hasn’t been filled in correctly.

For non-emergency health and safety repairs, it should take 3 to 12 months for the Department of Growth, Development and Defence Industries to review your funding application and let you know if your funding application is approved. It can take longer if more information is needed or if your application hasn’t been filled in correctly.

## Cost

There is no cost to apply for the funding.

## Before you start

Make sure you:

- review detailed eligibility criteria in the Housing Repair and Accessibility Program Guide
- know your total household income
- have the Canada Revenue Agency tax slips for the tax year before the year you’re applying for
- have the Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment for the tax year before the year you’re applying for
- have property tax bills and receipts showing that your property taxes are paid in full
- have all property owners and household members 19 or older available to sign the application form
- complete the Confirmation of Medical Condition Form, if applicable
- complete the Non-resident Homeowner Declaration Form, if applicable

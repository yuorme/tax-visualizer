Do you have a low income?
Do you rent or own your home?
Do you have at least one dependent
child or are you 50 or older?
You could get up to $170/month
to help you pay for housing.
There are two ways to apply for the shelter allowance:
1. Apply online in My Account for individuals
at revenuquebec.ca.
2. Download the required forms
at revenuquebec.ca/shelter-allowance-program.
Sign up for direct deposit!

and the Canada Mortgage Housing Corporation.
ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS
October 1, 2026, to September 30, 2027
Family situation Maximum
family income1

50 or over
$35,140

Single-parent family with one

Single-parent family with three

1. For individuals, couples and families that spend 80% or more
of their family income on housing. Note that you may not be
eligible even if your income is less than the maximum.
Do you receive or pay child support?
We may calculate your annual income differently
than in your income tax return (line 275).
For more information on the calculation and
other program requirements, go to
revenuquebec.ca/shelter-allowance-program.

The indexed official application explicitly identifies household income as the sum of line 15000 from each Notice of Assessment or Notice of Reassessment.
The contributing people are property owners and each owner's spouse residing at the property.
This is an older 2023 form; it supports the historical total-income definition, not a claim that the 2025 or 2026 form prints line 15000.
The downloaded 2026 form still requests prior-year Notices of Assessment and resident owners/spouses but does not explicitly name line 15000.

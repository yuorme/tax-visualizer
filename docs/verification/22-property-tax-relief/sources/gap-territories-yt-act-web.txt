Section 1: qualifying applicants paid the year's taxes or hold a seniors tax-deferral agreement not in default.
Residence can satisfy either normal residence from January 1 to application or at least 184 days in the tax year.
Eligible taxes exclude local improvements, arrears, penalties, delinquent taxes, fees and interest.
Section 2(2): ordinary grant is the least of 450 dollars, half eligible taxes, and tax minus 100 dollars if tax is below 200 dollars.
Section 2(3) overrides 2(2): qualified applicant must produce evidence of being at least 65; there is no OAS eligibility condition. Senior grant is the lesser of 500 dollars and three quarters of eligible taxes.
Section 2(4): surviving spouse of someone who received the senior grant uses the senior calculation if not remarried.
Section 3 sets February 15 following the relevant year as application deadline; payment/proof of full tax payment accompanies it.
Sections 4–5 restrict the claimant and residence to one annual grant, subject to the change-of-residence provision.
Section 7 separately permits payment of a deceased eligible applicant's grant to heir or surviving spouse.
Section 9 allows residence periods at successive properties to combine under its conditions.

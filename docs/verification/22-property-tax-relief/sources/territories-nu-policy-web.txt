Municipal council may exempt part or all of an eligible owner-occupier's property taxes by bylaw.
Senior: reaches 65 at any time in the relevant taxation period.
Eligible property includes residential units and qualifying land owned or leased by the unit owner.
Taxes exclude arrears, local improvements and interest.
Municipalities submit complete applications with tax notices and proof of eligible recipients.
GN funding is conditional on budget and acceptance of the application.
No fixed recipient percentage, dollar ceiling or income threshold is set in this policy.

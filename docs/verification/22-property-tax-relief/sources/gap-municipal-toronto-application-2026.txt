Relief Program Eligibility Criteria
Page 2 of 2
Application
2026 Property Tax, Water & Solid Waste Relief Programs
For Low-Income Seniors and Low-Income Persons with Disabilities
• Household income includes the combined income of all owners who reside at the property and their spouses, who
each must provide an unaltered copy of their current Notice of Assessment or Notice of Reassessment from the
Canada Revenue Agency (CRA).
• To be excluded from the household income an owner needs to provide proof they live elsewhere or documentation supporting
an extenuating circumstance (e.g., death certificate, in trust-beneficial owner, legal separation/divorce documents).
• Seniors must meet the age requirement by December 31, 2026.
• All owners living at the property, except the applicant’s spouse, must meet the eligibility criteria.
• Property taxes must not be outstanding in prior years and the utility account must be paid up to date.
• The property must be your principal residence, owned and occupied for at least one year before October 31, 2026.
• New application required each year and only one application can be made per household.

Complete this form if you are applying for the grant for seniors to offset
a municipal tax increase for 2026 (line 462 of your 2025 income tax return).
The purpose of the grant is to help offset a municipal tax increase brought
about by a significant increase in the value of your residence.
To apply for the grant, you must meet all the conditions below.
•• On December 31, 2025, you:
– were resident in Québec;
– were 65 or over; and
– had owned your residence for at least 15 consecutive years (note
that this period can include a period during which your spouse
owned the residence before transferring ownership to you).
•• Your residence is an entirely residential assessment unit consisting of
only one dwelling, and it serves as your principal place of residence.
(If your residence is a rectory that is partially or totally exempt from
municipal taxes under the
Act respecting municipal taxation, you
cannot apply for the grant.)
•• You received (or were entitled to receive) a municipal tax bill in your
name for the residence for 2026. (If you co-owned the residence, the
municipal tax bill may have been issued in another co-owner’s name.)
•• Your family income for 2025 (the amount on line 275 of your income
tax return plus, if you had a spouse on December 31, 2025, the amount
on line 275 of their return) was $64,200 or less.1
1. If you or your spouse on December 31, 2025, was not resident in Canada for the entire year, you must include in your family income all income that you and your spouse earned, including
all income earned while you were not resident in Canada.
To be entitled to the grant, you must also meet at least one of the following
two conditions:
•• An amount corresponding to the potential grant determined further to
the current assessment roll is shown on either your 2026 municipal tax
bill or the document entitled
Amount of the Potential Grant to Offset
a Municipal Tax Increase that was issued by your municipality.
•• A grant was awarded to you or to a co-owner of the residence in the
last year concerned by the previous assessment roll.
If you co-owned your residence on December 31, 2025, you and the other
co-owner(s) can split the amount of the grant. In such a case, each co-owner
must file a separate copy of this form.
Even if a municipal tax bill was issued in your name for 2026, you will stop
being eligible for the grant if you no longer own your residence on the
billing date shown on the 2026 bill or if the residence is no longer your
principal residence.
Complete this form and enclose it with your 2025 income tax return. Do not
enclose your municipal tax bill (or a copy of it); you must, however, keep it
for at least six years in case we ask for it.
Be sure to read the information on page 3 before completing this form.

General information
Spouse on December 31, 2025
The person who:
•• was your spouse at the end of that day and from whom you were not
living separate and apart at that time; or
•• was your spouse at the time of their death in 2025, provided you had
not been living separate and apart at that time and you did not have
a new spouse on December 31, 2025.
You are considered to have been living separate and apart only if your
separation was due to the breakdown of your relationship and lasted
90 days or more.
Transfer of ownership between spouses
If your spouse transferred ownership of your residence to you, you are
considered to have owned the residence the entire time your spouse was
the owner (or considered to own the residence due to the property rights
of your spouse’s previous spouse).
Review of the value of the residence
If the potential grant determined by the municipality for 2026 changes
because the value of your residence is reviewed, you will receive a new
municipal tax bill or another copy of the document entitled
Amount of
the Potential Grant to Offset a Municipal Tax Increase showing the adjusted
amount of the potential grant for 2026. If this happens after you apply for
the grant, you must request an adjustment to your 2025 income tax return
within 60 days. To do so, complete a
Request for an Adjustment to an
Income Tax Return (form TP-1.R-V) or use the online service for requesting
changes to your income tax return. You must also complete another copy
of this form and enter the new potential grant for 2026 on line 41.
Application deadline
To receive the grant for a given year, you must apply by December 31 of
the fourth year following the year in question. For example, to receive it
for 2026, you must apply by December 31, 2030.

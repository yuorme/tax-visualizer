The Affordable Access Program allows qualified individuals to submit a single application for three
municipal subsidized programs, at one time. Residents can complete this fillable form to apply to: the
Recreation Access Program, the Affordable Access Transit Pass Program and the Property Tax
Exemption and Deferral Program. If you require help with your application, please call 311.
Please select each of the programs you are applying for:

Proof of financial need must be included with your application and be one of the following:
Current Notice of Assessment from the Canada Revenue Agency showing total income
for all household (family unit)** members (18+);
a letter from the Nova Scotia Department of Opportunities and Social Development;
a referral from a community professional who is qualified to assess financial need and
is associated with a recognized community-based agency; or
a valid Welcomed in Halifax Card (WIH) with a photo ID.
Recreation Access Program
Please note:
All applicants must have an online Recreation account before submitting an application.
To set up an account, visit halifax.ca/myrec or a municipally owned recreation centre.
Household (family unit)** includes all family members living in the same residence and
are related by blood, marriage, common-law or adoption.
Affordable Access Transit Pass Program
Have gross household (family unit)** annual household income of $59,000*** or less for
2026/27 and include proof of financial need with your application as one of the following:
Current Notice of Assessment from the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) showing total
income for all household (family unit)** members 18 years or older;
proof of income statement (option ‘cʼ print) from the CRA showing total income on line
15000;
a letter from the Nova Scotia Department of Opportunities and Social Development; or
a referral from a community professional who is qualified to assess financial need and
is associated with a recognized community-based agency.
Please note:
Transit passes are for adult monthly passes only (ages 18+).
Applicants must not currently be enrolled in the provinceʼs Community Bus Pass Program
or the Student UPass Program (part of tuition package).

Program privacy statement
In accordance with Section 485 of the Municipal Government Act (MGA), any personal information
collected in this application will only be used by municipal staff and, if necessary, individuals under
service contract with the Halifax Regional Municipality for purposes relating to the administration of
the Affordable Access Program and the municipal subsidized programs it supports.
If you have any questions about privacy related topics, please email privacy@halifax.ca, access
related topics, please email at access@halifax.ca. For information on this form and the Affordable
Access Program, please call 311.

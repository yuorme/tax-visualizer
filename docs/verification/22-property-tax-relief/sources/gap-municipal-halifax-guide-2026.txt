What is the Affordable Access Program?
The program allows qualified individuals to submit a single application for three municipal
subsidized programs, at one time. Residents can complete the fillable form to apply to: the
Recreation Access Program, the Affordable Access Transit Pass Program and the Property
Tax Exemption and Deferral Program. If you require help with your application, please call 311.
PROGRAM
OVERVIEW
Provides partial or full coverage of recreation program fees for children, youth and
adults in financial need.
DEADLINE(S)
No deadlines but you must reapply annually. Note: If submitting a Notice of
Assessment (NOA) from Canada Revenue Agency as your proof of financial need,
the most recent NOA must be included with your application, effective July 1 each
year.
IMPORTANT
DETAILS
Once your application has been approved, the discount is applied to your account
for one calendar year from the approval date. No reimbursements. If you have
already paid, you can apply for assistance for next season. Note: Parks &
Recreation has the right to limit program access, when necessary.
WHAT YOU
RECEIVE
25%, 50% or 100% off all recreation programming at municipally owned recreation
facilities and at select multi-district facilities (Canada Games Centre, Cole Harbour
Place, St Margaretʼs Centre or Zatzman Sportsplex).
Excluding: private lessons, parties, facility rentals, programs offered by third-
parties, drop-in classes or personal training.
Note: At multi-district facilities, only one month memberships (renewable/paid in
full) are eligible for financial assistance.
ELIGIBILITY
CRITERIA
You are a resident of the Halifax region.
Have gross household (family unit)** income per the Statistics Canadaʼs 2026
Low income cut-offs (LICOs) before taxes table (Appendix A, Section i) and can
provide proof of financial need.
You have not yet paid for recreation programming.
Note: Household (family unit)** includes all family members living in the same
residence and are related by blood, marriage, common-law or adoption.
DOCUMENTS
REQUIRED TO
PROCESS
APPLICATION
If you meet the eligibility criteria above, you must include proof of financial need
with your application for all household members (18+) as one of the following:
Current Notice of Assessment from the Canada Revenue Agency;
a letter from the Nova Scotia Department of Opportunities and Social
Development;
a referral from a community professional who is qualified to assess financial
need and is associated with a recognized community-based agency; or
a valid Welcomed in Halifax Card (WIH) with a photo ID.
Recreation Access Program

PROGRAM
OVERVIEW
Qualified applicants can buy a monthly adult transit pass at a discounted price
(50% off regular price) for one year.
DEADLINE(S) Applications are accepted beginning mid-May each year and are processed on a
first come first served basis. The program is open from July to end of June of the
following year.
IMPORTANT
DETAILS
Applicants must include their verified HFXGO account email address in the
household table found in section 2 of the application form. Discounted passes can
be purchased through the HFXGO fare payment app or in person at a full-service
municipal customer service centre. Learn more at halifax.ca/hfxgo.
WHAT YOU
RECEIVE
A discounted (50% off) monthly adult transit passes for one year. Note: applicants
who choose to purchase and use the HFXGO app for their discounted passes,
must purchase all future passes on the HFXGO app.
ELIGIBILITY
CRITERIA
Have gross household (family unit)** annual household income of $59,000*** or
less for 2026/27.
Note:
Household (family unit)** includes all family members living in the same
residence and are related by blood, marriage, common-law or adoption.
Applicants must not currently be enrolled in the provinceʼs Community Bus
Pass Program or the Student UPass Program (part of tuition package) or hold a
valid Welcomed in Halifax card.
$59,000*** is the maximum household (family unit)** income as outlined in
Table T (Appendix A, Section ii).
DOCUMENTS
REQUIRED TO
PROCESS
APPLICATION
If you meet eligibility criteria noted above, you must include proof of financial need
with your application for all household members (18+) as one of the following:
Current Notice of Assessment from the Canada Revenue Agency;
proof of income statement (Option 'C') from the Canada Revenue Agency
showing total income on line 15000;
a letter from the Nova Scotia Department of Opportunities and Social
Development; or
a referral from a community professional who is qualified to assess financial
need and is associated with a recognized community-based agency.
NOTE: Incomplete applications will be automatically denied.

PROGRAM
OVERVIEW
The municipality can help homeowners pay property tax through a payment plan, a
deferral of property tax (payment moved to later date) or a deferral of local
improvement charges.
DEADLINE(S)
The program accepts applications from June 1 to December 31 each year.
Final tax bill exemption: August 15 each year.
Current year/interim tax bill exemption: December 31 of each year for an
exemption applied to the current year or to next yearʼs interim bill.
IMPORTANT
DETAILS
A mobile homeowner can apply for a rebate or payment plan but not a deferral.
A home includes a condo, mobile home or duplex.
For mixed-use properties, only the residential portion can be used to calculate
the rebate value.
Commercial or resource value is separate and not eligible for this program.
Under Administrative Order 10, NET World Income must be declared.
WHAT YOU
RECEIVE
Rebate and/or a deferral of payments on your tax bill per Table T (Appendix A,
Section ii).
ELIGIBILITY
CRITERIA
Have a gross household* income per Table T (Appendix A, Section ii) each year.
Your principal residence is located within the Halifax region and has a maximum
taxable property accessed value of $425,000 for the current year.
Must be the registered owner or co-owner of the residential property.
Application must name each resident of the residence.
Note: Household* includes all family unit members living in the same residence
(related by blood, marriage, common-law or adoption) and other residents (e.g.
roommates, etc).
DOCUMENTS
REQUIRED TO
PROCESS
APPLICATION
If you meet eligibility criteria noted above, you must include proof of financial need
with your application for all household* members (18+) as one of the following:
Current Notice of Assessment from the Canada Revenue Agency; or
proof of income statement (Option 'C') from the Canada Revenue Agency
showing total income on line 15000.
Applicants must include the following (as applicable):
a Schedule T1, for any NET World income
a statement of rental activities for any rental income from a boarder/renter (not
claimed on CRA Tax Return).
A business statement from your Income Tax Summary for any working from
home/self-employed claims.

Appendix B: Information about provincial programsHome repairs grants (to apply): Contact Housing Nova Scotia Central Regional
Office at 902.424. 5110 (or toll free: 1.800.774.5130.
Major home repair programs
Disabled Residential Rehabilitation Assistance Program (Disabled RRAP): Aids modify
homes occupied or intended to be occupied by persons with disabilities. Assistance is
available to low-income homeowners and landlords who provide affordable housing to low-
income households where a member of the household has a disability. A forgivable loan of up
$16,000 for homeowners. Note: income limit for program eligibility varies depending on the
location of the home and applicantʼs household size.
Home Adaptation for Seniorsʼ Independence Program (HASI): Provides financial assistance
of up to $3,500 to modify/adapt the homes of low-income seniors who are at least 65 years of
age to enable them to continue to live independently in their own homes. Assistance is
available to low-income senior homeowners. Note: The limit for program eligibility varies
depending on the homeʼs location and applicantʼs household size – assistance is a one-time
non-repayable contribution.
Homeowners Residential Rehabilitation Assistance Program (Homeowners RRAP): Provides
a forgivable loan of up to $16,000 to qualifying homeowners who own and occupy
substandard housing, to make repairs so that the home will meet at least minimum health and
safety standards. The amount of assistance available depends on the cost of the repairs,
household income and household size. The income limit for eligibility varies, depending on
location and household size.
Minor home repair programs:
Access-A-Home Program: This program provides grants of up to $5,000 to low to moderate
income households who must adapt their homes for wheelchair use. Often the grant pays for
wheelchair ramp, but it can also be used for widening doorways and hallways or making
bathrooms wheelchair accessible. The amount of the grant available depends on the
applicantʼs household income level. The maximum annual income limit for eligibility is $39,000.
Also, the applicant must submit valid medical documentation.
Emergency Repair Program: Assists eligible homeowners who live in rural areas to carry out
repairs that are needed on an urgent basis to deal with health and safety hazards. Examples
include repairs to unsafe wiring and heating systems and installing new wells and septic
systems. The maximum grant available is $6,000. The income limit for eligibility varies,
depending on locations and household size.
Housing Emergency Repair Program: Provides grants of up to $6,500 to lower income
households to carry out emergency, health and safety related repairs to their homes. The
annual income limit for eligibility varies, depending on location and household size.
Senior Citizens Assistance Program: Provides grants of up to $6,500 to seniors, who are at
least 65 years of age, to carry out emergency, health and safety related repairs to their homes.
The annual income limit for eligibility varies, depending on location and household size.

Appendix B: Information about provincial programs (continued)
Property Tax Rebate for Seniors Program:
This provincial program is for seniors who have been receiving the Guaranteed Income
Supplement in January of the application year. Criteria require you to have paid your property
taxes from the prior year in full. A receipt showing that taxes are paid in full upon approval will be
issued to the province. Applicants to this program receive a rebate on half of their taxes up to a
maximum of $800. Applications are accepted from July 1 to December 31 when the program
closes. Applications must be postmarked by December 31 and are available from Service Nova
Scotia by calling 902.424.5200 (toll free at 1.800.670.4357) or at any Access Nova Scotia Centre.
Learn more at provinceʼs program website.
Public Housing:
Contact the Metropolitan Regional Housing Authority (Halifax) at 902.420.6000
Repayable loan programs
Parent Apartment Program: Provides low-interest loans to homeowners for additions or
renovations to a single detached dwelling to create affordable housing accommodation for
senior family members. The maximum loan available is $25,000 and the maximum repayable
term is 10 years. The senior or seniors must be at least 50 years of age with a combined income
of $20,000 or less.
Small Loans Assistance Program: Provides low-interest loan assistance to help low to modest
income homeowners to undertake home repairs/renovations. This applicantʼs gross annual
household income must be less than $35,000 and the maximum loan available is $20,000.

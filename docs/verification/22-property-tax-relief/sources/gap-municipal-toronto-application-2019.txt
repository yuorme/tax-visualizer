Program Requirements
For all Property Tax, Water and Solid Waste Relief Programs
• Applications can only be submitted for a principal residence (i.e. the property where you reside).
• In order to verify program eligibility, the City requires that applicants provide an unaltered copy of their Notice of Assessment
(NOA) or Notice of Reassessment (NOR).
• Total income for all property owners and their spouses is used to determine eligibility for household income.
• Age requirements for tax, water and/or solid waste relief programs must be met by December 31, 2019.
• Each owner or their spouse must meet the eligibility criteria in order to qualify for the program.
• Property taxes must be paid in full as billed for all previous years. We recommend paying your current year’s taxes by the due
dates to avoid late payment charges.
• The Applicant must complete and submit to the City this rebate application and supporting documents.
• The Applicant must apply each year and only one application per household can be made annually.
The City will protect your personal information from unauthorized collection and use. For more information, visit
toronto.ca/accessingcityinformation
Property Tax Relief Programs
• The amount of the deferral or cancellation of property tax must be $10 or more.
For Property Tax Increase Cancellation Program:
• Household income must be $41,228 or less, AND household phase-in assessment value must be $975,000 or less.
• Be 65 years of age or older, OR be 60 to 64 of age and be in receipt of an Allowance under the Old Age Security Act; if widowed,
be in receipt of Spouse’s Allowance under the Old Age Security Act. OR
• Be a person living with a disability and be in receipt of disability benefits.
For Property Tax Deferral Program:
• Household income must be $50,000 or less, with no household assessment threshold.
• Be 65 years of age or older, OR be 60 to 64 of age and be in receipt of an Allowance under the Old Age Security Act; if widowed,
be in receipt of Spouse’s Allowance under the Old Age Security Act. OR
• Be 50 years of age or older and be in receipt of either a pension or a pension annuity resulting from a pension plan under the
Income Tax Act (Canada) OR
• Be a person living with a disability and be in receipt of disability benefits.
Water Rebate and Solid Waste Rebate Programs
• Household income must be $50,000 or less.
• Utility bills for the current year must be paid in full as billed.
• Be 65 years of age or older, OR be 60 to 64 of age and be in receipt of an Allowance under the Old Age Security Act; if widowed,
be in receipt of Spouse’s Allowance under the Old Age Security Act. OR
• Be 50 years of age or older and be in receipt of either a pension or a pension annuity resulting from a pension plan under the
Income Tax Act (Canada) OR
• Be a person living with a disability and be in receipt of disability benefits.
For Water Rebate Program
• The Applicant must consume less than 400m3 (cubic metres) annually. The City will remotely read all automatic meter reader
accounts after December 31, 2019.
For Solid Waste Rebate Program
• The Applicant must have a small, medium or large solid waste collection bin
• If eligible for solid waste rebate, a single rebate per household will be applied to your household’s largest size bin
Documents Needed for Application
Seniors
If you are applying for any of the property tax, water and/or solid waste rebate programs, these documents must be included with
your application as income verification for each owner and each spouse of an owner:
A copy of your 2018 or 2017 unaltered Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) income tax Notice of Assessment (NOA)* or Notice of
Reassessment (NOR)*
Proof of age is also required, if you are a senior and are applying for the first time. A copy of a birth certificate or driver’s
license may be submitted.
For the Property Tax Increase Cancellation Program, you must also include:
A copy of your Old Age Security T4A-OAS, OAS Allowance and/or Spousal Allowance T4A (if applicable)
For the Property Tax Increase Deferral Program, Water Rebate and/or Solid Waste Rebate Program, you must also
include:
A copy of your Old Age Security T4A, OAS Allowance and/or Spousal Allowance T4A (if applicable)
Proof of receipt of a registered pension or registered annuity under the Income Tax Act (Canada) (if applicable)
Persons with a Disability
If you are applying for any of the property tax, water and/or solid waste rebate programs, these documents must be included with
your application as income verification for each owner and each spouse of an owner:
A copy of your 2018 or 2017 unaltered Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) income tax Notice of Assessment (NOA)* or Notice of
Reassessment (NOR)*
A copy of your 2019 Disability benefit income documents from one or more of the following:
• Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP)
• Canada Pension Plan (CPP) (if your 2019 information is not available, submit 2018)
• Workplace Safety and Insurance Board (WSIB)
• Employment Insurance Sickness Benefit
• Motor Vehicle Accident Insurance
• Private Disability Insurance Plan
• Employer Disability Insurance Plan
• Disability programs provided by Professional Associations
If you do not have the above income documents, you should provide documentation that shows other financial support(s) or
circumstances in respect of your disability for special consideration.
* A Notice of Assessment or Notice of Reassessment is a document sent from the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) confirming
your income tax return for a specific year.

The page accepts 2026 applications through February 15, 2027.
Principal-residence property taxes may be municipal or rural territorial taxes.
Ordinary grant: 50%, maximum $450, subject to a $100 minimum tax.
Age 65+: 75%, maximum $500.
Applicants must own the home, pay the taxes in full, and live there for 184 days in the tax year.
One grant annually. Mortgage-company applicants can submit by June 10 so the lender pays tax net of grant.
Direct payment can deduct the grant at the time of payment.

The indexed official application is labelled 2025 Property Tax, Water and Solid Waste Relief Programs.
Household income includes resident owners and their spouses; non-resident owners can supply proof they live elsewhere.
Supporting documents explicitly require 2024 Notices of Assessment for the 2025 application, for resident owners and spouses.
Age requirement is met by December 31, 2025; all resident owners except the applicant's spouse must meet eligibility criteria.
The property must be the principal residence, owned and occupied for at least one year; an annual application is required.

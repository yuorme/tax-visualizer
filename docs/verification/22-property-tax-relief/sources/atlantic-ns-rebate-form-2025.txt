2026 PROPERTY TAX REBATE FOR SENIORS
This program provides a 50% rebate of residential municipal property taxes paid for 2025, up to $800.
To be eligible: • your 2025 municipal residential property taxes must be paid in full. The taxes must
be in your name, or include your name, and
• you must be at least 65 and receive, or be eligible to receive, the Guaranteed
Income Supplement (GIS) in 2026, or
• be between 60-64 and receive, or be eligible to receive, the Allowance in 2026.
The Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS) and the Allowance are monthly federal supplements
available to low-income Old Age Security pensioners. Contact Service Canada for more information.
To apply: • Complete the application in full. An incomplete application may delay your
rebate.
• Include a municipal property tax bill and receipt showing 2025 taxes paid in full.
Rebates are calculated on the total owed after municipal exemptions are applied.
All applications must be postmarked no later than December 31, 2026.
 Mail to: Property Tax Rebate for Seniors, Box 283, CRO, Halifax, NS, B3J 2N7
 Drop off: your nearest Access Nova Scotia

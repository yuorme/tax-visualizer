# Credits, Incentives and Benefits

The Government of Newfoundland and Labrador offers a number of tax credits, incentives and benefits programs to individuals and businesses.

## Individual Programs

- Residential Construction Rebate Program
- NL Income Supplement and NL Seniors Benefit
- Newfoundland and Labrador Child Benefit
- Political Contributions Tax Credit
- Venture Capital Tax Credit
- Low Income Tax Reduction
- Physical Activity Tax Credit
- Newfoundland and Labrador Disability Benefit

## Information respecting these programs may be obtained from the Tax and Fiscal Policy Branch.

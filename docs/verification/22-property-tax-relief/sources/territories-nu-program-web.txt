General taxation area excludes Iqaluit; the city administers its own property taxes.
People turning 65 during the taxation period, and eligible people with disabilities, may apply for exemption.
An owner or part-owner must ordinarily live in the eligible property. Application is required each taxation year.
The Finance Minister may exempt eligible general-area property.
The page supplies a 2026 tax-rate sheet and annual exemption/renewal applications.
It does not specify an income test or a universal fixed exemption percentage/cap.

The official table identifies the Home Owners Grant Act as RSY 2002 c110.
The sole listed amendment is section 3, by SY 2009 c21 section 6.
No amendment to the section 2 dollar amounts, senior override or surviving-spouse rule is listed.
The table includes 2026 amendments to other statutes and states that RSY 2002 Acts took effect January 1 2003.
The companion government consolidation likewise identifies only the 2009 amending Act.
This establishes the statutory section 2 schedule for 2025 and 2026, rather than deriving 2025 by an inflation assumption.
The standalone 2025 city notice records that Home Owners Grant applications were being accepted in May 2025.
That notice does not itself print dollar amounts, so its contents must not be represented as a dated dollar table.

The Senior Citizens and Disabled Persons Property Tax Relief program in Yellowknife offers property tax relief to seniors and people with disabilities.

### Eligibility

Seniors aged 65 and older, or people with disabilities, can apply for property tax relief.

### Application

Seniors must apply annually for the program, if they share the dwelling with another person.

### Tax Relief

Relief amounts vary based on the type of property owned or occupied.

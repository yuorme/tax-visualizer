# Property Tax Assistance Program

# Property tax assistance

Note: Our program opens on June 1 and applications must be submitted by Dec. 31, 2026. Due to the large volume of referrals, applications will be processed in 4-6 weeks.

If you are a residential property owner experiencing financial hardship, regardless of age, you may be eligible for a credit/grant of the increase on your property tax account.

## You may also qualify for

## Apply for our Fair Entry property tax assistance program

### Criteria

As an applicant, the following criteria must be met to be eligible for this program:

- You must be the owner on title for the subject property and must have been on title for a minimum of 365 concurrent days by the end of the subject taxation year.
- If there are multiple owners on the title for the subject property, all owners must be listed on the application, owners on title who are not applicants may be required to provide proof of alternate residence.
- You and any other property owner(s) on the application must be an individual/natural person and not be or represent a company.
- You must not own any other property within the City of Calgary.
- The subject property must show a year-over-year increase in its property tax levy.

### Restrictions

The subject property is not eligible for property tax assistance if a property’s value increased due to:

- physical changes
- external non-market impacts (owner-initiated zoning change, servicing, remediation, change in tax status, factual corrections, a change in assessment class)
- a supplementary assessment
- a partial development assessment

Apply now, through Fair Entry, and your one application can also get you access to additional subsidized programs and services. Even if you are not eligible for the Property Tax Assistance Program we may be able to help you access other resources. For more information about our programs call 311.

Please note: Applying for this program does not alter your property tax obligation. The 2026 property tax bill due date is June 30.

For more information about our programs visit, property tax, or Tax Instalment Payment Plan (TIPP), call 311 or 403-268-CITY (2489) if calling from outside Calgary.

All information submitted for property tax assistance is handled in a confidential manner. Personal information is collected in accordance with Section 33(c) of the Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act (FOIP).

Saskatchewan Tax Rates -> 2020 and 2021 Tax Rates -> Saskatchewan Personal Income Tax Rates Saskatchewan 2021 and 2020 Personal Marginal Income Tax Rates The Federal and Saskatchewan tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts were increased for 2021 by an indexation factor of 1.01. Saskatchewan's 2020 Budget reinstated indexation starting in 2021, using the federal indexation factor. Saskatchewan's 2021 Budget reduced the dividend tax credit for 2021 as a result of the reduction in the small business corporate income tax rate. The indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency information. Tax Tip: read the article Understanding the Tables of Personal Income Tax Rates, especially if you are trying to compare the rates below to the marginal tax rates in the Basic Tax Calculator.

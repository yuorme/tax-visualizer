British Columbia Tax Rates -> 2020 and 2021 Tax Rates -> BC Personal Income Tax Rates BC 2021 and 2020 Personal Marginal Income Tax Rates BC Income Tax Act s. 4.1, 4.3, 4.52, 4.69 The Federal tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2021 by an indexation factor of 1.010. The BC tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2021 by an indexation factor of 1.011. The indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency information. The BC 2020 Budget added a new high tax bracket for 2020 for taxable incomes over $220,000, which is indexed after 2020. Tax Tip: read the article Understanding the Tables of Personal Income Tax Rates, especially if you are trying to compare the rates below to the marginal tax rates in the Basic Tax Calculator.

Alberta Tax Rates -> 2020 and 2021 Tax Rates -> Alberta Personal Income Tax Rates Alberta 2021 and 2020 Personal Marginal Income Tax Rates Alberta Personal Income Tax Act s. 6.1, 8, 21, 44 The Federal tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2021 by an indexation factor of 1.010. The indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency information. As per the Alberta 2019 Budget, the Alberta brackets and tax credit amounts are not increased after 2019. If Alberta had not discontinued indexing, the Alberta tax brackets and personal amounts would have been increased for 2021 by an indexation factor of 1.013. June 29, 2020 Recovery Plan indicates that the dividend tax credit rate for eligible dividends will be adjusted January 1, 2021 corresponding with the acceleration of the general corporate income tax rate reduction. The legislative change for this is included in Bill 35, Tax Statutes (Creating Jobs and Driving Innovation) Amendment Act, 2020, which received Royal Assent December 9, 2020. See the Alberta 2020 Recovery Plan for more information, or see Alberta dividend tax credits. Tax Tip: read the article Understanding the Tables of Personal Income Tax Rates, especially if you are trying to compare the rates below to the marginal tax rates in the Basic Tax Calculator.

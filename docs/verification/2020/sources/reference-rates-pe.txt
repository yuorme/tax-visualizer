Atlantic Provinces -> Prince Edward Island Tax Rates -> 2020 and 2021 Tax Rates -> Prince Edward Island Prince Edward Island 2021 and 2020 Personal Marginal Income Tax Rates The Prince Edward Island tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are not increased for inflation. The Federal tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2021 by an indexation factor of 1.010. The indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency information. The PEI 2020 Budget increased the basic personal amount to $10,500 for 2021, with a proportional increase to the spouse/equivalent to spouse amount. The non-eligible dividend tax credit was reduced from 2.74% to 1.96% of the taxable dividend. If Prince Edward Island used indexing, their tax brackets and personal amounts would have been increased for 2021 by an indexation factor of 1.005. Tax Tip: read the article Understanding the Tables of Personal Income Tax Rates, especially if you are trying to compare the rates below to the marginal tax rates in the Basic Tax Calculator.

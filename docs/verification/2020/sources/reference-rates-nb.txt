Atlantic Provinces -> New Brunswick -> Tax Rates -> 2020 and 2021 Tax Rates -> New Brunswick New Brunswick 2021 and 2020 Personal Marginal Income Tax Rates New Brunswick Income Tax Act s. 14, 16.1, 19, 35 The Federal and New Brunswick tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2021 by an indexation factor of 1.010. New Brunswick uses the Federal indexation factor. On May 11, 2021 New Brunswick tabled Bill 48, An Act to Amend the New Brunswick Income Tax Act, which reduces the lowest personal tax rate from 9.68% to 9.40% effective January 1, 2021. The indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency information. Tax Tip: read the article Understanding the Tables of Personal Income Tax Rates, especially if you are trying to compare the rates below to the marginal tax rates in the Basic Tax Calculator.

Manitoba -> Tax Rates -> 2020 and 2021 Tax Rates -> Manitoba Personal Income Tax Rates Manitoba 2021 and 2020 Personal Marginal Income Tax Rates Manitoba Income Tax Act s. 4, 4.1, 4.6, 4.7 The Manitoba income tax brackets and basic personal amount are indexed, which began in 2017, but other Manitoba personal tax credits are not indexed. The Manitoba brackets and basic personal amount are increased for 2021 by an indexation factor of 1.010. The Federal tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2021 by an indexation factor of 1.010. The indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency information. Tax Tip: read the article Understanding the Tables of Personal Income Tax Rates, especially if you are trying to compare the rates below to the marginal tax rates in the Basic Tax Calculator.

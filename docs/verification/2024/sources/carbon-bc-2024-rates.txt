# Carbon tax rates by fuel type

Last updated on May 23, 2025
[image: warning]

Carbon tax was eliminated effective April 1, 2025.

See Carbon tax elimination for transitional information about refunds, special rules for natural gas sellers, what to do if you collect the tax in error and more.

The motor fuel tax is still in effect.

This page provides the current B.C. carbon tax rates. Find the past rates on our Past carbon tax rates page.

Find the scheduled carbon tax rates to 2030 in the Carbon Tax Act, Schedule 1 and 2.

Carbon tax rates for liquid fuels

Fuel type | Rate per unit April 1, 2024 to March 31, 2025
Gasoline | 17.61 cents per litre
Light fuel oil (includes diesel, locomotive fuel, heating oil and industrial oil) | 20.74 cents per litre
Heavy fuel oil | 25.50 cents per litre
Aviation fuel | 19.59 cents per litre
Jet fuel | 20.65 cents per litre
Kerosene | 20.65 cents per litre
Naphtha | 18.03 cents per litre
Methanol | 8.78 cents per litre

Carbon tax rates for gaseous fuels

Fuel type | Rate per unit April 1, 2024 to March 31, 2025
Natural gas | $3.9859 per gigajoule or 15.25 cents per cubic metre
Propane | 12.38 cents per litre
Butane | 14.24 cents per litre
Ethane | 8.15 cents per litre
Gas liquids | 13.31 cents per litre
Pentanes plus | 14.24 cents per litre
Refinery gas | 17.18 cents per cubic metre
Coke oven gas | 5.60 cents per cubic metre

Carbon tax rates for solid fuels

Fuel type | Rate per unit April 1, 2024 to March 31, 2025
Low heat value coal | $141.80 per tonne
High heat value coal | $178.48 per tonne
Coke | $254.38 per tonne
Petroleum coke | 30.18 cents per litre

Carbon tax rates for combustibles

Fuel type | Rate per unit April 1, 2024 to March 31, 2025
Peat | $81.76 per tonne
Combustible waste (includes whole and shredded tires and asphalt shingles) | $159.78 per tonne

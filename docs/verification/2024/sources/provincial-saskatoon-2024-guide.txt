Each year your property tax bill consists of three components:
Education tax is collected to provide funding for the public and separate school divisions.
Municipal tax is invested in services we all rely on such as police/fire protection, road maintenance,
transit, snow management, parks, recreation and more.
Library tax is collected to fund the Saskatoon City Library programs and services.
Tax Questions?
Have other questions about property tax or assessment? Visit our website saskatoon.ca/propertytax or
call 306-975-2400. We're here to help.
PROPERTY TAX INFORMATION - Page 4
Explanation of Terms:
Assessed Value: The value of a property as of a given
base date determined by using legislative guidelines.
Deferred Tax: The tax amount to be paid at a later date
for approved applicants.(Seniors Property Tax Deferral
Program).
Taxable Assessment: The Province determines the
percentage of value (POV) that is taxable by a
municipality. The Taxable Assessment for a Residential
property is based on 80% of its Assessed Value; for
Commercial properties it's based on 85% of the
Assessed Value.
Lien Year: The year a tax lien was registered on the title
of the property.
Tax Rate: Mill Rate/1000 x Mill Rate Factor
(Mill Rate Factor is not applied to Education Tax).
Previous Balance: Payments, arrears, or other
charges added to the tax account prior to issuing the
tax notice. Arrears are related to a prior tax year and
subject to tax enforcement.
Other Charges (if applicable):
Tax Phase-In is complete for the 2021
reassessment cycle.
Special Charges are for encroachments and/or
awnings that extend onto City property.
Bid Levy is an additional tax on commercial
properties collected on behalf of each BID
(Business Improvement District).
Late Payment Penalty:
Payment received after June 30 of the current tax year will have a late payment penalty of 1.75% per month
applied.
If you pay your property tax after June 30 of the current tax year, call 306-975-2400 to confirm the amount due,
including penalties.
If your tax account is not paid in full by December 31 of the tax year, a 2.25% monthly penalty will be assessed
and the property becomes subject to The Tax Enforcement Act.
Seniors Tax Payment Assistance
Are you a low-income senior citizen age 65 or older who owns and resides in your own property in Saskatoon?
Consider applying for the Seniors Property Tax Deferral Program to defer payment of a portion of your
Municipal and Library Taxes for this year. To learn more about the deferral program and how to apply, visit
saskatoon.ca/propertytax or call 306-975-2400 (or 1-800-667-9944).

Education Property Tax Information:
The City of Saskatoon is bound by provincial legislation to bill and collect education property tax from Saskatoon
property owners.
The Province of Saskatchewan sets the mill rates for education property tax for all public school divisions.
The Saskatoon separate school division set their own mill rates and adopted the provincial mill rates.
The City does not keep education property tax for use in providing City services.
Information on the education property tax and mill rates can be found on the Province of Saskatchewan
website: saskatchewan.ca
To declare or change your school support, complete the form found at saskatoon.ca/propertytax.

Education Tax Questions Property Tax-Related Questions
How do I change my School Division support?
To change your School Division Support, complete
the Individual School Tax Declaration form at
saskatoon.ca/propertytax. No fee is required to
update school support or ownership.
I am a property owner without children.
Am I required to pay Education property tax?
Yes. Regardless of your family status, The Cities Act
requires all property owners to pay the full amount of
property tax owing to all three taxing authorities as
shown on your tax notice.
For questions about my Education property tax,
who should I contact?
Contact the Ministry of Government Relations
at 1-306-787-2653 or visit their website
saskatchewan.ca and search Education tax.
Can I look up the exact amount I owe for
my property taxes using the online Property
Assessment & Tax Tool?
No. The information found using the online tool
does not contain the full details of your specific tax
account; it shows the tax levy in relation to your
property’s assessment.
It does not include other charges such as
BIDS, phase-in and special charges. Please call
306-975-2400 for any tax-related questions.
Can I appeal any portion of my property tax?
No. Only your property’s assessment can be
appealed during the annual Assessment Review
and Appeal Period.
How do I have my new legal name displayed
on my Property Tax Notice?
You must submit an application and an affidavit to
ISC. Forms and instructions are available at isc.ca.
The City of Saskatoon’s Assessment and Property Tax
records reflect the exact name as registered at the
provincial ISC office (Land Titles).
Your 2024 Property Tax payment
is due June 30, 2024.
For payments made after June 30, 2024, the property will be
assessed a 1.75% monthly penalty. Late payment penalties are
added to the tax account, and compound monthly.

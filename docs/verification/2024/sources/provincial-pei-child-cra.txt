# Province of Prince Edward Island

[image: Prince Edward Island flag]

The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) administers the following programs for the Province of Prince Edward Island:

- Program related to the Canada child benefit (CCB):

- Prince Edward Island child benefit

- Program related to the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB) (formerly known as the goods and services tax/harmonized sales tax (GST/HST) credit):

- Prince Edward Island essentials benefit

[image: Prince Edward Island flag]

## Prince Edward Island child benefit

The Prince Edward Island child benefit (PEICB) is a tax-free amount paid to help low- to medium-income families with the cost of raising children. This amount is combined with the CCB into a single monthly payment.

For July 2026 to June 2027, you may be eligible to receive:

- $34.16 per month for each child under 18 years of age if your adjusted family net income is less than $45,000;
- $24.16 per month for each child under 18 years of age if your adjusted family net income is between $45,000 and $80,000.

If your adjusted family net income is greater than $80,000, the amount is reduced to zero.

This program is fully funded by the province of Prince Edward Island.

## Prince Edward Island essentials benefit

The Prince Edward Island essentials benefit (PEIEB), which replaces the Prince Edward Island sales tax credit starting November 2026, is a program that provides additional financial support to help cover essential household expenses.

### Who is eligible

You are eligible for this benefit if:

You are a resident of Prince Edward Island (PEI) on the first day of the payment issuance quarter

You are at least 19 years old

### How much you may receive

The amount you may receive depends on your adjusted family net income (AFNI) and your family status. Effective July 1, 2026, a minimum PEIEB amount of $175 was introduced, ensuring all PEI residents who qualify can receive the benefit, regardless of income.

### Amounts for single individuals for July 2026 to June 2027

Adjusted family net income (AFNI) | Annual amount
Up to $95,000 | $310
From $95,000 to $108,500 | From $175 to $310
Over $108,500 | $175

### Amounts for couples and single parents for July 2026 to June 2027

Adjusted family net income (AFNI) | Annual amount
Up to $95,000 | $365
From $95,000 to $114,000 | From $175 to $365
Over $114,000 | $175

### When to expect your payments

PEIEB payments are normally issued quarterly on the 5th of July, October, January and April, and combined with the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB) payments.

If the 5th of the payment month falls on a weekend or statutory holiday, the payment is issued on the last working day before the 5th of that month.

#### Payment schedule for the period of July 2026 to November 2026

For this period, the payment schedule depends on the annual amount of your PEIEB payment and your adjusted family net income (AFNI).

#### Single individuals

AFNI up to $108,500: July 2026, October 2026, January 2027, April 2027

AFNI from $108,500 to $126,000: July 2026, October 2026, November 2026

AFNI over $126,000: November 2026 only

#### Couples and single parents

AFNI up to $114,000: July 2026, October 2026, January 2027, April 2027

AFNI from $114,000 to $131,500: July 2026, October 2026, November 2026

AFNI over $131,500: November 2026 only

Note: The July and October 2026 payments were issued under the PEISTC program.

If your annual amount is $0 to $174.99, and you are eligible for the PEIEB, you will receive the minimum annual benefit of $175 in November 2026.

If you have recently moved to Prince Edward Island, you will begin receiving payments on the next available payment date. For example, if you move on January 10, 2027, you are not entitled to the January 2027 amount. You will receive your first payment on April 5, 2027.

In January 2027, the PEIEB will start following the regular CGEB payment schedule, i.e. quarterly in July, October, January and April.

#### Why your payments may stop

Your PEIEB payments may have stopped because:

- You didn’t file your tax return or filed late
- You didn't respond to a letter from the Canada Revenue Agency about your situation
- You are no longer a resident of PEI

#### How to receive your payments

You do not need to apply for this benefit.

The CRA will use the information we have on file to determine your eligibility. However, you must file your income tax and benefit return every year even if you have no income to report.

Once you have filed your return for the year, you should receive your first PEIEB payment on the next available payment date.

#### Contact the Canada Revenue Agency if you have questions

If you have additional questions about the PEIEB, contact the Canada Revenue Agency by calling the Benefit Enquiries line at 1-800-387-1193.

#### Policy enquiries

If you have a tax policy question about the PEIEB, contact the Government of Prince Edward Island at 1-902-368-4030.

## Adjusted family net income

If you have a spouse or common-law partner, your adjusted net incomes are combined to get your adjusted family net income (AFNI).

Your AFNI is:

Your family net income (line 23600 of your tax return, plus line 23600 of your spouse's or common-law partner's tax return, if applicable)
−
Minus any Universal Child Care Benefit (UCCB) and registered disability savings plan (RDSP) income received (line 11700 and line 12500 of your or your spouse's tax return, if applicable)
+
Plus any UCCB and RDSP amounts repaid (line 21300 and line 23200 of your or your spouse's tax return, if applicable)

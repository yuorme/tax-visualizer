Reducing the effect of the Northwest Territories Carbon Tax on
Individuals and Families
The Cost-of-Living Offset (COLO) reduces the carbon tax burden on residents and the benefit will
increase in step with annual carbon tax rate increases. Residents living in higher hea�ng fuel use
areas will receive higher COLO payments. The Canada Revenue Agency issues COLO payments
quarterly (July, October, January, and April) provided residents file their annual income tax returns.
The regional COLO has two parts:
1. Baseline COLO amount to help offset direct and indirect effects of carbon tax excluding
carbon tax on hea�ng fuel. At $65 a tonne carbon tax rate, the baseline COLO amount will
be $338 for each adult and $390 for each child under 18 years of age.
2. An addi�onal COLO amount to help offset the effect of the carbon tax on hea�ng fuel
costs and this amount will be based on zones. At $65 a tonne carbon tax rate, Zone A
residents will receive $103 per person; for Zone B residents: $145; and for Zone C
residents: $220.

# Home owner grant for seniors

Last updated on July 3, 2026

The home owner grant reduces the amount of property taxes you pay each year for your principal residence.

If you’re a senior aged 65 or older, your property is assessed at $2,075,000 or less and you meet certain requirements, you may qualify for a higher amount of the grant called the additional grant.

For properties located in B.C., including properties located in northern and rural areas, effective January 1, 2027, the total grant amount for seniors aged 65 or older is $845. The additional grant amount of $200 for northern and rural properties has been eliminated.

Properties assessed over $2,075,000 may receive a partial grant.

You must pay at least $100 in property taxes before claiming the home owner grant. These minimum amounts of property taxes fund services such as road maintenance and police protection.

## Do I qualify

To qualify for the additional grant:

You must

- Be 65 years of age or older in the current year
- Be the registered owner of the residence
- Be a Canadian citizen or permanent resident of Canada
- Live in B.C.
- Occupy the residence as your principal residence

The assessed or partitioned value of your property must not exceed the grant threshold

You must meet additional requirements if you are buying or selling your property

The homeowner who meets these qualifications must be named as the applicant to receive the additional grant for the property.

Exact excerpt:
"Beginning in the 2026 taxation year, the maximum age for an eligible child will be lowered from 16 to 14."

Paraphrase of the remaining eligibility conditions:
The new rule requires the child to be younger than 14 at some point during the year. It covers the taxpayer's or
spouse's child, or a dependant meeting the income limit, subject to the other eligibility conditions. The change
leaves the exemption from the age limit for dependent children with mental or physical infirmities intact.

The March 25, 2025 Quebec budget announced the change. The backend represents ordinary year-end ages through 14
in 2026, compared with through 16 in 2025, and retains infirm-dependant eligibility without an ordinary age cap.

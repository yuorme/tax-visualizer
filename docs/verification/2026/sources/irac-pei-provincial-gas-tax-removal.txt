NOTICE
PRICE ADJUSTMENT RELATED TO TEMPORARY SUSPENSION OF PROVINCIAL TAX
ON GASOLINE AND DIESEL FUELS
Charlottetown, Prince Edward Island, September 25, 2026 – The Prince Edward Island Regulatory and
Appeals Commission is advising petroleum consumers that the Government of Prince Edward Island has
announced amendments to the rate of tax imposed under the Gasoline Tax Act on gasoline and diesel sold
in Prince Edward Island effective September 26, 2026, As a result, there will be a price adjustment effective
at 12:01 AM on Saturday, September 26, 2026 to remove the provincial tax from these products.
The amendments to the rate of tax imposed under the Gasoline Tax Act include further adjustments to be
effective at future dates. The Commission will issue Orders in accordance with the amendments on such
dates.
The chart below summarizes the announced changes in the provincial tax portion on all grades of gasoline
and diesel fuel that are effective from September 26, 2026 until January 31, 2027.
CURRENT PROVINCIAL
TAX

Gasoline (All Grades) 8.47 cpl 0.0 cpl

For current petroleum prices visit irac.pe.ca.
The Commission’s next scheduled pricing adjustment is Tuesday, September 29, 2026.

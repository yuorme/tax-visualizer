Repayment resulting from death
30.3(1) Subject to subsection (2), no obligation to repay all or a
portion of a refund of overpayment made under section 30.2 arises
as a result of the death of a person who, but for the person’s death,
would be an eligible individual or qualified dependant for the
purposes of that overpayment.
(2) Subsection (1) does not apply with respect to a refund of
overpayment or any portion thereof if the refund or portion of the
refund arose as a result of the provision of false or misleading
information. 2019 c20 s9(23)

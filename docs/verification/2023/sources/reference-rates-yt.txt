# Yukon 2024 and 2023 Tax Rates & Tax Brackets

The Federal and Yukon tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2024 by an indexation factor of 1.047 (4.7% increase), except for the Yukon amount of $500,000, which is equal to the corporate small business limit.

Yukon uses the Federal tax brackets for their first 3 tax brackets.

The 2024 Federal Budget proposed an increase in the capital gains inclusion rate to 66 2/3% for the portion of gains realized after June 24, 2024 in excess of $250,000, for an individual. We've added an extra column below, to show the marginal tax rate for those gains. The inclusion rate is still 50% for the portion of gains up to $250,000 after June 24, 2024.

See Indexation of the Personal Income Tax System for how the indexation factors are calculated.

The indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates for 2024 have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency information.

Tax Tip: read the article Understanding the Tables of Personal Income Tax Rates, especially if you are trying to compare the rates below to the marginal tax rates in the Basic Tax Calculator or the Detailed Tax Calculators.

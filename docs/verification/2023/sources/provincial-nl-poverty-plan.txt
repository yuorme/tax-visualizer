Prenatal-Early Childhood Nutrition Supplement
CHANGE:
• Non-Taxable Benefit of $150 per month to assist with the extra costs of healthy eating during pregnancy
and up to the child’s first birthday
• Payments are made by CSSD until the child is born and then Government of Canada administers on
behalf of the provincial government as a portion of the Canada Child Benefit payment until the child is
one year old
• Expand the program to age five providing $150 per month for an additional four years
• Rename Prenatal Infant Nutrition Supplement to the Prenatal - Early Childhood Nutrition Supplement to
better reflect the intention of the program to provide nutritional support during pregnancy and children in
their early years
7
IMPACT:
• An additional 2,000 families will receive the benefit

NL Child Benefit
CHANGE:
• Non-taxable benefit paid monthly to help with the cost of raising children (<18 years)
• Annual Rates - One Child - $447, Two Children - $921, Three Children - $1430, and $547 for every
additional child
• Increase the NLCB by 300% with the initial increase by March 2024 and retroactive to January 2024
• New annual rates by January 2024 - One Child - $1788, Two Children - $3684, Three Children - $5720,
and $2188 for every additional child
8
IMPACT:
• Families of 14,000 children will receive increased benefits

--- PDF page 12 of 17 ---

Streamline & Improve Income Support Benefits
CHANGE:
• Income Support provides basic financial benefits and other services to eligible people to assist with
basic living expenses, based on an assessment of all household income and assets
• The program structure is complex with more than 30 different basic monthly rates that are based on
household composition and living arrangements, as well as additional amounts that require
documentation from an approved professional
12
• Streamline and improve Income Support benefits to provide financial supports that are improved and
simplified in a more timely manner
• Improved and simplified benefits: Fewer separate benefit rates that are standardized and at
higher levels
• Increased flexibility: Benefit amounts less dependent on household composition and living
arrangements
• Improved ease of access: Reduced requirements for documentation
IMPACT:
• Increased benefits and reduced poverty for the most vulnerable people

• People with long-term requirements for supportive services to live independently are among those who
experience the highest barriers to employment, and often require ongoing financial supports.
• Currently, financial supports are provided through Income Support and the NL Health Services

• Implement a targeted basic income for people age 60 to 64 currently receiving basic monthly financial
supports through the Income Support program and the NL Health Services’ Community Support
Program, at an amount equal to the basic Federal seniors benefits, which will result in more adequate,

• A targeted basic income will provide higher monthly incomes through a single source, eliminate

Employment Stability Pilot
• The recently expanded Employment Stability Pilot provides enhanced incentives and supports for
participants receiving Income Support who begin a new job and/or maintain and increase their hours of work
• The pilot provides opportunities for people in receipt of Income Support to immediately realize increased
financial benefits from working through an increased overall monthly income and experience increased
financial rewards for entering and remaining in the workforce
• The pilot provides people with financial stability needed to ease the transition from Income Support to work
CHANGE:
• Continue to work with community partners to complete independent evaluation
• Assess whether to implement evaluation recommendations
• Promote incentives and supports to encourage labour market participation
15
IMPACT:
• Increased workforce attachment and higher overall incomes for Income Support recipients

--- PDF page 16 of 17 ---

CHANGE:
Non-EI eligible training support program
• Provide a skills training program to support people who are not EI eligible to attend post-secondary
programming that will lead to employment in the same manner as is currently available for people who are
EI eligible, including funding for living allowance, childcare, and costs of training
• Expand existing enhanced referral and assessment process for Income Support Social Workers to refer
Income Support recipients
• Training will prioritize post-secondary programs with entrance requirements suitable to upskilling people
with no previous post-secondary, history of low paying jobs and connections to Income Support
• Currently, people who are not eligible for Employment Insurance (EI) do not have access to robust
employment and training programs to assist with retraining or upskilling in the same manner as
those who are EI eligible
• A program to encourage, retrain or upskill people who are non-EI eligible to fill workforce shortages
16
IMPACT:
• Increased skills development to support long-term attachment to the labour market

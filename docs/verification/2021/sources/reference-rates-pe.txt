Home -> Atlantic Provinces -> Prince Edward Island Tax Rates -> 2021 and 2022 Tax Rates -> Prince Edward Island Prince Edward Island 2022 and 2021 Personal Marginal Income Tax Rates The Prince Edward Island tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are not increased for inflation. The Federal tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2022 by an indexation factor of 1.024 (a 2.4% increase). The indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency information. The PEI 2021 Budget increased the basic personal amount to $11,250 for 2022, and Bill 37 legislated an increase to the spouse/equivalent to spouse amount. Bill 37 also reduced the non-eligible dividend tax credit rate. If Prince Edward Island used indexing, their tax brackets and personal amounts would have been increased for 2022 by an indexation factor of 1.033 (a 3.3% increase). Tax Tip: read the article Understanding the Tables of Personal Income Tax Rates, especially if you are trying to compare the rates below to the marginal tax rates in the Basic Tax Calculator.

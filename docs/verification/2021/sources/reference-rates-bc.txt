Home -> British Columbia Tax Rates -> 2021 and 2022 Tax Rates -> BC Personal Income Tax Rates BC 2022 and 2021 Personal Marginal Income Tax Rates BC Income Tax Act s. 4.1, 4.3, 4.52, 4.69 The Federal tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2022 by an indexation factor of 1.024 (a 2.4% increase). The BC tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2022 by an indexation factor of 1.021 (a 2.1% increase). The indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency information.

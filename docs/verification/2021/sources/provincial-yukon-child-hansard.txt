Paraphrased data transcription of Finance Minister Currie Dixon's response:
Maximum Yukon child benefit, each child annually, 2015/16 through 2022/23: $820.
Full amount for family net income below $35,000; eligible families above this receive a reduced amount.
Indexation to Whitehorse CPI began July 2023, when the annual maximum became $876.

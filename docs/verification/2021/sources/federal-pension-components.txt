## Calculation of CPP maximum monthly amounts for new benefits

Retirement:
25% of 1 over 12 of the average year's maximum pensionable earnings (YMPE) for the last 5 years.

Disability:
(retirement x 0.75) + flat amount ($510.85).

Survivor:

- younger than 65: (retirement x 0.375) + flat amount ($199.31)
- 65 and older: retirement x 0.60

Post Retirement:
1 over 40 of the retirement benefit.

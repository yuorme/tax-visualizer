# Yukon 2023 and 2022 Tax Rates & Tax Brackets

The Federal and Yukon tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2023 by an indexation factor of 1.063 (6.3% increase), except for the Yukon amount of $500,000, which is equal to the corporate small business limit.

Yukon uses the Federal tax brackets for their first 3 tax brackets.

See Indexation of the Personal Income Tax System for how the indexation factors are calculated.

The indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates for 2023 have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency information.

Tax Tip: read the article Understanding the Tables of Personal Income Tax Rates, especially if you are trying to compare the rates below to the marginal tax rates in the Basic Tax Calculator or the Detailed Tax Calculators.

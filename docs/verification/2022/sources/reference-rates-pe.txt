# Prince Edward Island 2024, 2023 and 2022 Tax Rates & Tax Brackets

The Prince Edward Island tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are not increased for inflation.

If Prince Edward Island used indexing, their tax brackets and personal amounts would have been increased for 2023 by an indexation factor of 1.084 (8.4% increase).

The Federal tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2023 by an indexation factor of 1.063 (6.3% increase).

See Indexation of the Personal Income Tax System for how the indexation factors are calculated.

The indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates for 2023 have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency information.

The PEI 2023 Budget increased the 2023 basic personal amount to $12,750, and it will be increased to $13,500 for 2024. There will also be additional tax brackets and revised tax rates for 2024, and the 10% surtax has been eliminated.

The official release says direct-deposit payments start July 5, 2022 and confirms the passed Bill 64.
It separates the ordinary sales-tax credit, carbon rebate, and extraordinary inflation support.

Published examples (income is annual family income):
- Single, $45,000: regular quarterly credit $41.25; carbon increment $140; inflation support $150.
- Single, $55,000: regular quarterly credit $16.25; carbon increment $115; inflation support $150.
- Single parent, $45,000: regular quarterly credit $55; carbon increment $140; inflation support $300.
- Couple, $63,000: regular credit zero; carbon increment $65; inflation support $300.

Only the carbon increment is implemented by the new carbon program. The separately modeled recurring sales-tax
credit remains outside this increment, and extraordinary inflation support is excluded from this project scope.

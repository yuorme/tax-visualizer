# Quebec 2023 and 2022 Tax Rates & Tax Brackets

The Quebec tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2023 by an indexation factor of 1.0644 (6.44% increase).

The Federal tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2023 by an indexation factor of 1.063 (6.3% increase).

See Indexation of the Personal Income Tax System for how the indexation factors are calculated.

The federal indexation factor, tax brackets and tax rates for 2023 have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency.

The Quebec indexation factor, tax brackets and tax rates for 2023 have been confirmed to Quebec Ministry of Finance information. The Quebec 2023 Budget on March 21, 2023 reduced the tax rate for the bottom 2 tax rates, and reduced the tax rate for personal tax credits from 15% to 14%.

The Quebec basic personal amount is rounded to the nearest $1, beginning with the 2018 taxation year. The Quebec tax thresholds are rounded to the nearest $5. 2017 is the base year for the thresholds and the personal amount.

The marginal tax rates below include the reduction in rate due to the federal tax abatement of 16.5% of federal tax. However, this abatement is applied to the net federal tax after deducting the basic personal amount. For this reason, the federal personal amount tax credit is also reduced by 16.5%. The Federal personal amount shown below is before the reduction from the federal tax abatement.

Tax Tip: read the article Understanding the Tables of Personal Income Tax Rates, especially if you are trying to compare the rates below to the marginal tax rates in the Basic Tax Calculator or the Detailed Tax Calculators.

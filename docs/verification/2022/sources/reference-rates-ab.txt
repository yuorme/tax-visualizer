# Alberta 2023 and 2022 Tax Rates & Tax Brackets

##### Alberta Personal Income Tax Act s. 6.1, 8, 21, 44

The Alberta tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2023 by an indexation factor of 1.06 (6% increase).

The Federal tax brackets and personal tax credit amounts are increased for 2023 by an indexation factor of 1.063 (6.3% increase).

See Indexation of the Personal Income Tax System for how the indexation factors are calculated.

On August 31, 2022, Premier Jason Kenney announced as part of the 2022 Q1 Update that indexation of the personal tax system would be restored, retroactive to January 1, 2022. The 2022 brackets were updated using an indexation factor of 1.023 (2.3% increase).

The federal and Alberta indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates for 2023 have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) information.

Tax Tip: read the article Understanding the Tables of Personal Income Tax Rates, especially if you are trying to compare the rates below to the marginal tax rates in the Basic Tax Calculator or the Detailed Tax Calculators.

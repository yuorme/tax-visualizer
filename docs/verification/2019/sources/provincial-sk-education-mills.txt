EDUCA 164/19. HOLMES: That council acknowledge the 2019 confirmed Education
MILL property tax mill rates, from Minister of Government Relations
Agriculture 1.43 mills
Residential 4.12 mills
Commercial/Industrial 6.27 mills
Resource 9.68 mills. CARRIED.
MEPP 165/19. HOLMES: That the Rural Municipality of Meeting Lake pay their portion
BEREAVE of MEPP contributions for bereavement pay. CARRIED.
BEAVER 166/19. TAYLOR: That council authorizes licensed individuals – Buck Scotton, Troy
Pomedli, Garry Panchuk, Maurice Allchurch, Connor Holmes, Otto Lavallee, Jack
VanDyke, Rod Allan, Curtis Babiy, Jack Pool, Brain Jacobson, Carson Toews, Cole
Brochu, Darrell Pollard, Cody Zyznomirski and Glen Bachelder - that hold a valid
trapping license to deliver the beaver tails to one of the following individuals, Bob
German, Earl Holmes and Jack Pool for confirming the beaver tails. The deadline
to receive the beaver tails is June 12, 2019. The individual that receives the beaver
tails must be marked with paint or a notch so they cannot be re-used for any other
program. CARRIED.
CHANNEL 167/19. ALLCHURCH: That application be made for financial assistance under the
CLEAR provisions of the Channel Clearing Assistance for R.M.’s Program, through Water
Security Agency with proposed expenditures to be a total of $40,000.00. CARRIED.
ACCOUNT 168/19. HOLMES: That attached list of accounts, cheques numbered 6631 to 6664
APPROVA and direct deposits 930-939 in the amount of $ 68,711.77 be approved for payment.
CARRIED.
ADJOURN 169/19. TAYLOR: That this meeting adjourn at 3:52 p.m. CARRIED.

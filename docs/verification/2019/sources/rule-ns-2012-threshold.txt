N.S. Reg. 148/2012
Made: July 24, 2012
Filed: July 24, 2012
Nova Scotia Child Benefit Regulations
Order in Council 2012-229 dated July 24, 2012
Amendment to regulations made by the Governor in Council
pursuant to Section 80 of the Income Tax Act
The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Finance dated June 14, 2012, and
pursuant to Section 80 of Chapter 217 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Income Tax Act, is
pleased to amend the Nova Scotia Child Benefit Regulations, N.S. Reg. 62/98, made by the Governor in Council
by Order in Council 1998-430 dated August 28, 1998, to increase child benefit amounts, in the manner set forth
in Schedule “A” attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation, effective on and after July 1,
2012.

Atlantic Provinces -> Newfoundland & Labrador -> Tax Rates -> 2019 and 2020 Tax Rates -> Newfoundland & Labrador Newfoundland & Labrador 2020 and 2019 Personal Marginal Income Tax Rates Newfoundland and Labrador Income Tax Act, 2000 s. 6.1, 7, 9 Please read the article Understanding the Tables of Personal Income Tax Rates. The Federal tax brackets and personal amounts are increased for 2020 by an indexation factor of 1.019, and the NL brackets and amounts are increased by an indexation factor of 1.009. The indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency information. The tax rates do not include the NL Deficit Reduction Levy, which can add 10% to the marginal tax rate at various levels of taxable income over $50,000. 2020 is the last year for this levy, unless legislation is tabled to extend it. See the effect of the Deficit Reduction Levy by using our Detailed Canadian Tax and RRSP Savings Calculator.

Saskatchewan Tax Rates -> 2019 and 2020 Tax Rates -> Saskatchewan Personal Income Tax Rates Saskatchewan 2020 and 2019 Personal Marginal Income Tax Rates Please read the article Understanding the Tables of Personal Income Tax Rates. The Federal tax brackets and personal amounts are increased for 2020 by an indexation factor of 1.019. Saskatchewan amounts are no longer indexed. The indexation factors, tax brackets and tax rates have been confirmed to Canada Revenue Agency information. If Saskatchewan had not discontinued indexing, the Saskatchewan tax brackets and personal amounts would have been increased for 2020 by an indexation factor of 1.017.

Marginal tax rate for dividends is a % of actual dividends received (not grossed-up taxable amount). Marginal tax rate for capital gains is a % of total capital gains (not taxable capital gains).

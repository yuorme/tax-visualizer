Education property tax credit
You must complete the declaration for the education property tax credit below if you are claiming rent and/or school taxes paid
for the education property tax credit.
The Income Tax Act of Manitoba allows only one education property tax credit per residence. If you shared accommodation
with one or more individuals, only one of you can claim an education property tax credit for that residence for the time you
were sharing.

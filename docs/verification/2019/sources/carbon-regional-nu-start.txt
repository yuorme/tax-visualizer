Federal carbon tax began July1,2019. Government of Nunavut offsets50% of the federal levy at the point of sale. The initial FAQ describes a proposed future reduction in rebate support; later2022 release confirms50% remained effective then. Do not implement the initial planned decline without checking subsequent decisions.

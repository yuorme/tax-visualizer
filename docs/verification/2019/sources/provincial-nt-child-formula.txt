Northwest Territories Child Benefit
What is the Northwest Territories Child Benefit?
The Northwest Territories Child Benefit program (NWTCB) provides benefits to families
based on the number of children and their ages, and family net income declared in the
previous year’s income tax return.
Starting July 1, 2017, the NWTCB was enhanced to provide more non-taxable benefits to
low income families with children under the age of eighteen.
Who is eligible to receive the NWTCB?
Families with annual net income under $30,000 will receive the full child benefit. The
benefit will start to be phased out when family income exceeds $30,000, and will be
eliminated when family income reaches $80,000. The structure of the NWTCB is shown in

How is the NWTCB calculated?
The annual NWTCB is calculated as follows:
If A is less than or equal to B:
NWTCB = C + D
If A is greater than B:
NWTCB = (C + D)
Where:
1. A is family net income
2. B is the minimum income threshold of $30,000
3. E is the maximum income threshold of $80,000
4. C and D are benefit levels shown in the following table:

How do I apply to receive the NWTCB?
The NWTCB continues to be administered by the Canada Revenue Agency, and is included
with monthly Canada Child Benefit payments.

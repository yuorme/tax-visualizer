N.S. Reg. 74/99 ROYAL GAZETTE Part II Regulations
N.S. Reg. 74/99
Made: June 24, 1999
Filed: June 25, 1999
Vital Statistics Appointment of Division Registrars Regulations
Order in Council 1999-347 made June 24, 1999
Regulations made by the Governor in Council
pursuant to clauses 51(1)(b) and (p)
of the Vital Statistics Act
The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of
Business and Consumer Services dated June 16, 1999, pursuant to clauses 51(1)(b)
and (p) of Chapter 494 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Vital
Statistics Act, is pleased to make regulations respecting the appointment of division
registrars in the form set forth in Schedule “A” attached to and forming part of the
report and recommendation, effective on, from and after July 1, 1999.
Schedule “A”
Regulations respecting the appointment of division registrars
for births, stillbirths and deaths made by the Governor in Council pursuant
to Section 51 of Chapter 494 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia,
the Vital Statistics Act
Citation
1 These regulations may be cited as the Vital Statistics Appointment of Division
Registrars Regulations.
Division registrars - deaths
2 (1) Every person who holds
(a) a valid funeral director’s licence issued prior to July 1, 1999 by the
Nova Scotia Board of Embalmers and Funeral Directors; and
(b) a certificate of good standing issued by the Deputy Registrar General
is appointed as a division registrar for deaths.
(2) A person who holds a valid funeral director’s licence issued by the Nova
Scotia Board of Embalmers and Funeral Directors after July 1, 1999, may
apply to the Deputy Registrar General for a certificate of good standing
and, upon receipt of the certificate, is appointed as a division registrar for
deaths.
3 (1) When, in the opinion of the Deputy Registrar General, the holder of a
certificate of good standing issued pursuant to Section 2 has not complied
with the provisions of the Act or regulations respecting the registration of
deaths, the Deputy Registrar General may

Deputy Registrar General sees fit; or
(b) withdraw the certificate of good standing.
(2) A person whose certificate of good standing has been withdrawn pursuant

4 (1) Every person who
(a) is employed as a nurse manager, health records technician, ward clerk

1999, may apply to the Deputy Registrar General for a certificate of good
standing and, upon receipt of the certificate, is appointed as a division
registrar for births and stillbirths.
5 (1) When, in the opinion of the Deputy Registrar General, the holder of a
certificate of good standing issued pursuant to Section 4 has not complied
with the provisions of the Act or regulations respecting the registration of
births and stillbirths, the Deputy Registrar General may
(a) suspend the certificate of good standing for such period of time as the
Deputy Registrar General sees fit; or
(b) withdraw the certificate of good standing.
(2) A person whose certificate of good standing has been withdrawn pursuant
to clause (1)(b) may apply to the Deputy Registrar General for a new
certificate no earlier than 1 year after the withdrawal, and shall present
with the application a letter from the hospital or other medical facility at
which the person is employed recommending that the person be issued a
new certificate.

Nova Scotia Child Benefit Regulations
Order in Council 1999-357 made June 28, 1999
Amendment to regulations made by the Governor in Council
pursuant to Section 34
of the Income Tax Act
The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of
Finance dated June 15, 1999, and pursuant to Section 34 of Chapter 217 of the
Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Income Tax Act, is pleased to amend the
Nova Scotia Child Benefit Regulations made by Order in Council 1998-430 dated
August 28, 1998, in the manner set forth in Schedule “A” attached to and forming
part of the report and recommendation, effective on, from and after the dates
specified in Schedule “A”.
Schedule "A"
Amendments to the Nova Scotia Child Benefit Regulations
made by the Governor in Council
pursuant to Section 34 of Chapter 217
of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989,
the Income Tax Act
1 Section 3 of the Nova Scotia Child Benefit Regulations made by the Governor in
Council by Order in Council 1998-430 dated August 28, 1998, is amended by
(a) striking out the formula “NSCB = 1/12 [(A + B +C) -D]” and
substituting “NSCB = [1/12 (A + B + C)] - D”; and
(b) striking out “A is $250” and substituting “A is $324”;
(c) striking out “B is $168” and substituting “B is $242”;
(d) striking out the formula “C = $136 x E” and substituting “C = $210 x
E”; and
(e) striking out “subsection 4(1)” and substituting “Section 4”.
2 Subsection 4(2) of the regulations is repealed and the following subsection
substituted:
(2) Where the adjusted income of the eligible individual for the base
taxation year in relation to the month is $16,000 or greater, D is equal
to the lower of
(a) the amount determined by the following formula:

# Last year tax rates and income brackets (2025)

- For income earned in: 2025
- Applied to: 2025 tax return (to be filed in 2026)

Provincial or territorial income tax rates apply in addition to federal income tax rates. The tax rates are split up into income brackets. Each rate applies only to the corresponding income bracket, not to all income.

## Federal rate for 2025

The rate is determined by the Government of Canada.

Federal rate: 2025
Each portion of your taxable income
from | up to | is taxed at
$0 | $57,375 | 14.5% Footnote 1
$57,375.01 | $114,750 | 20.5%
$114,750.01 | $177,882 | 26%
$177,882.01 | $253,414 | 29%
$253,414.01 | unlimited | 33%

Footnote 1

The federal tax rate decreased from 15% to 14% on July 1, 2025, making the tax rate 14.5% for 2025.

You want to stop making QPP contributions and you understand
that:
•• No future retirement pension supplement will be allocated

•• If your QPP or CPP retirement pension is cancelled, your election

Instructions
Québec Pension Plan (QPP) contributions
The QPP is a mandatory public insurance plan for workers 18 or over
whose yearly income is more than $3,500. You must contribute until
the year you turn 73, which means that you will not pay contributions
on the salary and earnings you receive as of January 1 of that year.
Part A – Section 2
Eligibility
You can elect to stop making QPP contributions if you meet all the
following conditions:
•• You are self-employed or a person responsible for a family-type
resource or an intermediate resource.
•• You were 65 or over but under 73 at the end of the year.
•• You receive a QPP or Canada Pension Plan (CPP) retirement pension.
•• You did not revoke an election to stop making QPP contributions
during the year.
You can make the election as of the day after your 65th birthday.
NOTE
It is usually better to continue making QPP contributions after you
turn 65. For more information, go to the Retraite Québec website
at retraitequebec.gouv.qc.ca.
Conditionsfortheelection
You can only complete this schedule onceayear. If you elect to stop
making QPP contributions one year, you cannot revoke the election
until the following year.
Example
If you elected to stop making QPP contributions in June, you will
have to wait until at least January 1 of the following year to revoke
your election and resume your QPP contributions.
Likewise, if you revoke an election you made in a previous year, you will
have to wait until the following year to make a new election to stop
making contributions.
Election
To make the election to stop making QPP contributions, complete this
schedule and file it with your income tax return for the year in which you
want the election to apply.
If you are self-employed or a person responsible for a family-type
resource or an intermediate resource, the election will apply to your
income from self-employment only. To make this election for your
employment income for the same year, complete form RR-50-V,
Election
to Stop Contributing to the Québec Pension Plan, or Revocation
of an Election, send it to us and give a copy to your employer(s).
Be sure to keep proof that you receive a QPP or CPP retirement pension
so that you can provide it on request.
Effectiveelectiondate
The election comes into effect on the first day of the month in which
you made it. We will stop calculating your QPP contribution as of the
first day of the election month.
Revocation
To revoke an election to stop making QPP contributions, complete
this schedule and file it with your income tax return for the year in which
you want the revocation to apply.
If you made an election during the year and you want to resume
your QPP contributions, you cannot revoke it untilyoufileyourincome
taxreturn for the following year.
Example
If you elected to stop making QPP contributions in June, you will
have to wait until at least January 1 of the following year to revoke
your election and resume your QPP contributions.
The revocation takes effect on the first day of the month you made
the revocation. We will calculate your QPP contributions as of the first
month covered by the revocation.

Part C
Optionalcontributiononcertaintypesofemploymentincome
If the total QPP and CPP contributions you made as an employee
in 2025 (lines 98, 98.2, 96 and 96.2 of your return) were less
than $4,735.20 and you want to increase your QPP benefits, you can
make an additional contribution on all or part of the income reported
on line 107 and on certain income included on line 101 (see “Optional
contribution to the Québec Pension Plan (QPP)” in the instructions
for line 101). If you decide to make an additional contribution, check
box 444 on your return and complete Part C of this schedule.
However, if you entered amounts on lines 96, 96.1 and, if applicable,
96.2, complete form LE-35-V,
Contribution and Deduction Related
to the QPP or the CPP, instead of this schedule. Do not enclose the form
with your return, but keep it for your records.
You must request the optional contribution no later than June 15 of
the second year following the year for which you want to make
the contribution.
Indian
If you are an Indian, complete the following lines in Part C as follows:
•• On line 30, include the net business income entitling you to
a deduction on line 293 of your return.
•• On line 31, include your pensionable earnings as a person responsible
for a family-type resource or an intermediate resource that constitute
income situated on a reserve or premises.
•• On line 31.2, include the net business income entitling you
to a deduction on line 293 of your return and your pensionable
earnings as a person responsible for a family-type resource
or an intermediate resource that constitute income situated
on a reserve or premises (both included on line 31.1) for which you
arenotelectingto make QPP contributions.
•• On line 32, include the employment income entitling you to a
deduction on line 293 of your return, on which QPP contributions
were not withheld at source and on which you are making
an optional QPP contribution.
If all the employment or business income on which you are making
an optional contribution entitles you to a deduction on line 293
of your return, you cannot claim a deduction for the optional contribution.
Specialcases
•• If you are self-employed and all your business income entitles
you to a deduction on line 297 (points 7, 9 and 12), you cannot deduct
the contribution payable in respect of that business. If necessary,
we will correct the amount you enter on line 248.
•• If you areapersonresponsibleforafamily-typeresourceoran
intermediateresourceandyoureceivedanRL-29slip, you cannot
claim a deduction for the contribution payable on the earnings
you entered on line 40 of Schedule L. If you entered amounts
on lines 30 and 32 of Schedule U, contact us.

# Past carbon tax rates

Last updated on March 13, 2025

This page provides the past B.C. carbon tax rates from April 1, 2019 to March 31, 2024. For the current carbon tax rates, see our Carbon tax rates by fuel type page.

Carbon tax rates from April 1, 2019 to March 31, 2024
Fuel type | April 1, 2019 to March 31, 2021 | April 1, 2021 to March 31, 2022 | April 1, 2022 to March 31, 2023 | April 1, 2023 to March 31, 2024
Gasoline | 8.89 cents per litre | 9.96 cents per litre | 11.05 cents per litre | 14.31 cents per litre
Light fuel oil (includes diesel, locomotive fuel, heating oil and industrial oil) | 10.23 cents per litre | 11.71 cents per litre | 13.01 cents per litre | 16.85 cents per litre
Heavy fuel oil | 12.60 cents per litre | 14.36 cents per litre | 15.93 cents per litre | 20.72 cents per litre
Aviation fuel | 9.84 cents per litre | 11.21 cents per litre | 12.44 cents per litre | 15.92 cents per litre
Jet fuel | 10.44 cents per litre | 11.61 cents per litre | 12.91 cents per litre | 16.78 cents per litre
Kerosene | 10.44 cents per litre | 11.61 cents per litre | 12.91 cents per litre | 16.78 cents per litre
Naphtha | 10.20 cents per litre | 10.13 cents per litre | 11.27 cents per litre | 14.65 cents per litre
Methanol | 4.36 cents per litre | 4.95 cents per litre | 5.49 cents per litre | 7.14 cents per litre
Natural gas | $1.9864 per gigajoule or 7.60 cents per cubic metre | $2.3053 per gigajoule or 8.82 cents per cubic metre | $2.5588 per gigajoule or 9.79 cents per cubic metre | $3.2384 per gigajoule or 12.39 cents per cubic metre
Propane | 6.16 cents per litre | 6.98 cents per litre | 7.74 cents per litre | 10.06 cents per litre
Butane | 7.04 cents per litre | 8.01 cents per litre | 8.90 cents per litre | 11.57 cents per litre
Ethane | 3.92 cents per litre | 4.59 cents per litre | 5.09 cents per litre | 6.62 cents per litre
Gas liquids | 6.60 cents per litre | 7.52 cents per litre | 8.32 cents per litre | 10.81 cents per litre
Pentanes plus | 7.04 cents per litre | 8.01 cents per litre | 8.90 cents per litre | 11.57 cents per litre
Refinery gas | 7.04 cents per cubic metre | 12.15 cents per cubic metre | 13.50 cents per cubic metre | 13.96 cents per cubic metre
Coke oven gas | 6.44 cents per cubic metre | 3.15 cents per cubic metre | 3.50 cents per cubic metre | 4.55 cents per cubic metre
Low heat value coal | $71.08 per tonne | $79.74 per tonne | $88.62 per tonne | $115.21 per tonne
High heat value coal | $83.08 per tonne | $101.34 per tonne | $112.58 per tonne | $145.02 per tonne
Coke | $99.48 per tonne | $143.10 per tonne | $158.99 per tonne | $206.68 per tonne
Petroleum coke | 14.68 cents per litre | 17.28 cents per litre | 19.19 cents per litre | 24.52 cents per litre
Peat | $40.88 per tonne | $45.99 per tonne | $51.10 per tonne | $66.43 per tonne
Combustible waste (includes whole and shredded tires and asphalt shingles) | Shredded tires $95.64 per tonne, whole tires $83.20 per tonne | $89.87 per tonne | $99.87 per tonne | $129.82 per tonne

Note: Combustible waste category started April 1, 2021

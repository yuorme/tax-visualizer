DECISION AND ORDER M12057
NOVA SCOTIA UTILITY AND REVIEW BOARD
IN THE MATTER OF THE PETROLEUM PRODUCTS PRICING ACT
- and -
IN THE MATTER OF PRESCRIBING PRICES for Petroleum Products under Section
14 of the Petroleum Products Pricing Act and Sections 16 to 19 of the Petroleum Products
Pricing Regulations
BEFORE: Steven M. Murphy, MBA, P.Eng., Member
DECISION AND ORDER
Under s. 16 of the Petroleum Products Pricing Regulations, the Board must prescribe a
benchmark price for specified petroleum products every Friday. However, the Board may
prescribe a benchmark price any time, and due to significant changes in the price of
petroleum products, the Board has determined that it is appropriate to adjust its most
recently prescribed weekly prices.
The benchmark price prescribed by the Board is the average of the average of the daily
high and low reported product prices (in Canadian cents) since the last benchmark price
adjustment for the petroleum product.
Under s. 17 and 18 of the Petroleum Products Pricing Regulations, the Board must also
prescribe retail mark-ups and calculate wholesale margins considering any factors the
Board finds relevant, including transportation adjustments. The Board last set wholesale
margins in its Decision, 2023 NSUARB 154; retail mark-ups in its Decision, 2021
NSUARB 50; and transportation adjustments in its Decision, 2016 NSUARB 168.
On December 23, 2024, the Board issued an Interim Order, 2024 NSUARB 215 (Matter
M11978), increasing the wholesale margin for self-service and full-service gasoline and
diesel, on an interim basis, by 3.00 cents per litre (cpI), up to a total of 13.84 cpI for
gasoline and 14.84 cpI for diesel, respectively, effective 12:01 a.m. on January 3, 2025.
Section 17 of the Petroleum Products Pricing Regulations also requires that the fixed
wholesale price for each type of petroleum product include all taxes (taxes imposed under
subsections 165(1) and 165(2) of the Excise Tax Act (Canada); carbon pricing, any fuel
charges payable to His Majesty in right of Canada, a clean fuel adjustor amount set by
the Board under s. 17A to address the financial and administrative burdens associated

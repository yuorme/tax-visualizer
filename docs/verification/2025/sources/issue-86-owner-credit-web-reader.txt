The provincial credit adjusts the 2023 assessment toward its 2020 basis, with adjustments for later acquisition
and improvements. The 2024 assessment increase remains, up to 2.9%. Ownership must continue from December 31,
2023; properties purchased after that date do not qualify. Municipal increases are outside the credit.

If you and your spouse on December 31, 2025, are both entering an amount on line 455 of your respective returns, you must each file your own Schedule C.
To qualify for the tax credit, the childcare expenses paid must have been incurred while you or your spouse on December 31, 2025, was in any
of the situations listed below.

A child under 16 at some point in the year or
a child of any age with a mental or physical
infirmity who was your or your spouse’s
dependant.

•• your or your spouse’s child; or
•• a child who was your or your spouse’s
dependant and whose income for the year
was not more than $13,658 (the child’s
income is the amount on line 275 of their

have been entered on line 275 had the child
filed a return).

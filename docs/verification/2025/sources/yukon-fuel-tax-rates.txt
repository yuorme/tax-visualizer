# Find current fuel tax rates in the Yukon

## Current fuel tax rates in the Yukon

- Gasoline: 6.2 cents per litre.
- Diesel: 7.2 cents per litre.
- Aviation: 1.1 cents per litre.
- Heating: no tax.
- Heating or cooling other than for space heating: 0.2 cents per litre.
- Propane: no tax.

Last updated on April 5, 2018.

## How fuel tax rates are governed and enforced in the Yukon

All fuel purchases in the Yukon are governed by the Fuel Oil Tax Act and Regulations.

Read the Fuel Oil Tax Act and Regulations.

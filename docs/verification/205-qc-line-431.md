# Quebec line 431: age-independent credit transfer (#205)

## Rule confirmation

Confirmed before changing the engine: the cached TP-1 return subtracts non-refundable credits at line 413,
then dividend and other listed credits at line 430. A negative line 430 is transferable to a spouse on
line 431. The recipient's line 432 cannot be negative. The guide imposes no age or pension-receipt condition.

Primary evidence already cached in this repository:

- [2025 TP-1, page 4](138-senior-credit-rules/sources/qc-return-2025.txt), lines 401–432.
- [2025 guide, line 431](138-senior-credit-rules/sources/qc-guide-2025.txt), printed page 64:
  a negative line 430 may transfer to the December 31 spouse, who records it on line 431. Both returns must
  be filed. The split-income restriction is outside the model's supported income categories.

## Implementation and year coverage

Previously, age or retirement inputs activated the general transfer as a side effect of senior-credit allocation.
Without those inputs, only unused BPA transferred through the legacy spouse-credit slot, omitting unused DTC.
Every Quebec couple with personal credits enabled now uses the same line 431 calculation. The legacy provincial
spouse credit is zero, so BPA cannot be claimed through both paths. Federal spouse claims are unchanged.
The spouse-savings figure adds both adults' applied line-431 transfers to the existing federal/provincial spouse
relief. It counts the credit actually used, not the full unused entitlement. The Quebec-specific legacy transfer
label and row are removed; the provincial spouse/dependant/living-alone row still serves its other uses.
The golden-locked `pAmt`, `pCredit` and `provincialClaimant` fields remain for compatibility.

The general transfer reads existing gross tax, BPA and dividend credits. It does not read senior parameters.
Senior Schedule B allocation and Schedule 2 transfers still run only when senior inputs activate them.
The personal-credit switch retains its established meaning: it disables spouse transfers as well as BPA.
Unrelated adults cannot transfer. The calculation retains both transfer directions and the recipient tax cap.

## Independent calculations

For these ordinary Quebec returns, let `L = bracket tax - BPA credit - dividend credits` (line 430).
Each spouse receives `max(0, -L_other)` on line 431; line 432 is `max(0, L - transfer)`.
Before any foreign tax credit, household tax therefore equals `max(0, L_first + L_second)`.
Senior cases additionally include their allocated Schedule B credit in L; that allocation is unchanged.

### Issue example, 2026

First adult: 20,000 eligible dividends. Second adult: 40,000 other income plus 2,000 eligible dividends.
No age, deductions, spending or withholding are needed. Eligible gross-up is 1.38.

- BPA credit: `18,952 × .14 = 2,653.28` per adult.
- Donor: taxable income `20,000 × 1.38 = 27,600`, gross tax `3,864`, DTC `27,600 × .117 = 3,229.20`.
  Line 430 is `3,864 - 2,653.28 - 3,229.20 = -2,018.48`.
- Recipient: taxable income `42,760`, gross tax `5,986.40`, DTC `322.92`.
  Line 430 is `3,010.20`; line 432 is `3,010.20 - 2,018.48 = 991.72`.
- Federal donor tax is zero. Recipient federal tax is
  `(42,760 × .14 - 16,452 × .14 - 2,760 × .150198) × .835 = 2,729.2588892`.
- Health Services Fund contributions remain `15 + 150 = 165`: actual dividends enter the base, with the
  existing 18,500 threshold, 1% rate and 150 first-column cap.
- Total income tax plus contributions: `2,729.2588892 + 991.72 + 165 = 3,885.9788892`, displayed **3,885.98**.
  Previously **5,904.46** without age; adding age 40 gave **3,885.98**. Both now give **3,885.98**.

### Unused BPA must transfer once

In 2026, a zero-income donor and a recipient with 40,000 other income have Quebec gross tax of 5,600.
The recipient's own BPA leaves `2,946.72`; the donor transfers `2,653.28`; final Quebec tax is **293.44**.
Retaining the legacy spouse credit alongside line 431 incorrectly reduces this to zero.

A donor with 2,000 eligible dividends has gross tax `386.40`, unused BPA `2,266.88` and DTC `322.92`.
The transfer is `2,589.80`, leaving **356.92** for the same recipient. A BPA-only interpretation overstates tax.
The recipient-cap fixture instead gives the donor 20,000 eligible dividends and recipient 20,000 other income:
only the recipient's remaining tax can be used, even though the donor has more unused credits.

### Existing shared scenario 133

This is the only existing household whose current provincial tax changes in each year. Donor income is
8,000 employment, 2,000 eligible dividends and 3,000 foreign dividends. Recipient income is 47,000 employment,
5,000 capital gains, 2,000 eligible dividends and 20,000 foreign dividends, less 15,000 deductions.
Withholding is zero, so no FTC offsets the correction.

For 2026, donor net income is `13,760 - 45 enhanced QPP = 13,715`; subtracting the 480 worker deduction
gives 13,235 and tax of 1,852.90. Unused BPA is 800.38; adding DTC of 322.92 gives a transfer of 1,123.30.
Recipient taxable income is `57,260 - 435 enhanced QPP - 1,450 worker deduction = 55,375`.
Bracket tax is `54,345 × .14 + 1,030 × .19 = 7,804`.
Final provincial tax is `7,804 - 2,653.28 - 322.92 - 1,123.30 = 3,704.50`, formerly **4,027.42**.

For 2019, donor taxable income is 13,273.25 and tax 1,990.9875; BPA credit is 2,290.35.
The full transfer is `299.3625 + 325.128 = 624.4905`.
Recipient tax is `9,015.45 - 2,290.35 - 325.128 - 624.4905 = 5,775.4815`, formerly **6,100.6095**.
The reduction is exactly the donor's previously unusable eligible DTC: 325.128 in 2019, 322.92 thereafter.

### Savings shown to the household

The 2026 browser examples use a recipient with 60,000 employment income and a donor with zero or 18,000 other income.
For the zero-income donor, federal spouse relief is `16,452 × .14 × .835 = 1,923.2388` and the Quebec transfer is
`18,952 × .14 = 2,653.28`. The sum is **4,576.5188**, displayed **4,577**. Provincial taxable income is
`60,000 - 565 enhanced QPP - 1,450 worker deduction = 57,985`; bracket tax is
`54,345 × .14 + 3,640 × .19 = 8,299.90`. Subtracting both BPA credits gives **2,993.34**, unchanged by this review fix.
For the 18,000 donor, federal spouse relief is zero; unused Quebec credit is
`(18,952 - 18,000) × .14 = 133.28`, displayed **133**. The page no longer incorrectly says no spouse relief applies.
Both inputs are tested in both adult orders and with age omitted or age 40 entered.

All 206 savings cells identified by review were independently rechecked. An engine-free calculation recomputed
Quebec bracket tax from locked taxable income and raw yearly brackets, subtracted BPA and full DTC, and computed
each transfer as `min(max(0, L_recipient), max(0, -L_donor))`. It then added the unchanged federal spouse credit
after abatement. Of these cells, 203 changed from the reviewed commit; three correctly retain only federal relief
because both Quebec returns already have no tax left after their own credits. Exact comparison confirms that
the review changes no other existing golden field, including any tax, marginal rate or FTC value.

Against the PR base, 186 of the savings cells return to their original value. Eight increase by the newly usable
donor DTC. Twelve decrease because line 431 is capped at tax remaining after the recipient's own DTC; the old
pre-DTC spouse-credit slot overstated the transferable credit actually used. For example, the 2026 shared scenario
35 recipient has 1,855.50 left, so the transfer uses 1,855.50 of the donor's 2,653.28; savings are
`1,923.2388 + 1,855.50 = 3,778.7388`, not 4,576.5188. Shared scenario 80 has no Quebec capacity in 2024–2026,
so only federal relief remains. The household tax in all twelve cases is unchanged.

The strengthened switch-off case gives the donor 20,000 eligible dividends and a 20,000 deduction, and the recipient
40,000 other income. Without BPA, the donor still has `3,229.20 - 7,600 × .14 = 2,165.20` unused dividend credit.
With personal credits off it must not transfer: Quebec tax stays **5,600.00**. Removing the switch guard incorrectly
gives **3,434.80**, and now fails the regression assertion.

## Existing baseline audit

Compared all 148 shared scenarios across 13 jurisdictions for every year, plus all ten original other-income
scenarios in 2025 and 2026 with their frozen fuel/carbon context. Only Quebec couples change.
No existing input, assertion or tolerance was changed. Only independently audited leaf values were patched;
the fixtures were not recaptured wholesale. No other open PR was present when work began.

A case means one stored scenario in one year/province; a cell is one changed numeric leaf, including nested
person/claim/marginal fields. The 2025 and 2026 totals each include two cases and 10 cells from
`other-income-original.json`; the remaining changes are in the respective yearly fixture.
All other provinces, unrelated-adult households and single-adult households retain their exact baselines.
A one-person input with `adults: 2` is already modelled as a couple with a zero-income spouse and is included.

Of the 222 cases, eight change current tax, 25 change marginal rates without current tax, and 189 change only
credit allocation/bookkeeping. The legacy `pSp`, `pSpFull` and claim amount fields lose the BPA transfer now
represented in the nested line-431 breakdown; `claim.saved` includes the transfer actually applied.
Some applied DTC values increase because line 431
comes after the recipient's own DTC. These do not represent a separate tax-rule correction.

An independent temporary calculation imported no engine modules. It read raw yearly YAML and existing locked
payroll/credit inputs, recomputed net income, bracket tax, federal spouse relief and FTC capacity, then evaluated
the old BPA-only and corrected TP-1 paths. Together with the savings re-audit above, it checked all 1,037 final
changed cells by propagating the tax delta to
personal/household totals, averages, investment counterfactuals and next-100-dollar marginal counterfactuals.
Old snapshots have four decimals, so this delta audit allowed one last-place unit inherited from rounded inputs;
the committed golden assertions retain their existing exact comparisons. Maximum audit difference was 0.0001.

For example, a BPA donor's next 100 eligible dividends produces a transferable DTC of `138 × .117 = 16.146`:
the household marginal rate falls by .16146 when the recipient can use it. For non-eligible dividends the
corresponding 2026 reduction is `115 × .0342 / 100 = .03933`. Income-based benefits and federal tax are unchanged
between paths. Some foreign-dividend/capital-gain marginal values move by .0001 at a four-decimal rounding tie
because the transfer changes floating-point subtraction order; the independent tax delta is zero for those
counterfactual rates. These were diagnosed rather than treated as additional relief.

Cells combining effects are claim savings (federal saving plus either adult's applied line-431 credit), applied DTC
(own DTC precedes the incoming credit), and investment/marginal values (difference between corrected actual and
counterfactual returns). They were computed separately in both paths, not inferred by blindly adding the donor DTC.

## Regression and golden mapping

`tests/golden/features/quebec-line-431.json` adds 72 cases, nine per year, with 360 numeric expectations.
They were generated from the TP-1 equations and the published BPA/rate table above, independently of engine output.
All fixture incomes stay within the first bracket; eligible dividends use the 1.38 gross-up.
The independent generator is committed at `tests/golden/generate-quebec-line-431.mjs`:

```sh
fnm exec --using=26 node tests/golden/generate-quebec-line-431.mjs "$TMPDIR/qc-line-431-generated.json"
```

It requires a new output path and refuses to overwrite files. Its parsed output exactly matches the locked
72 cases and 360 expectations; Biome formats the committed JSON arrays more compactly.

The targeted suite first failed with `3010.2 !== 991.72`. It now passes 77 tests, including the savings regression
that first failed with `1923.2388 !== 4576.5188`.
Mutation checks separately restored the age gate, added the legacy BPA credit beside line 431, and disabled one
transfer direction, then removed the personal-credit switch guard. Each was rejected by an assertion in the
targeted suite; all mutations were removed. Browser assertions open the actual savings tooltip for both examples
at 375, 430 and desktop widths in light and dark mode, and check that only the current line-431 ledger row appears.

Territorial Amounts Transferred from your
Spouse or Common-Law Partner
Complete this schedule to claim a transfer of the unused part of your spouse's or common-law partner's territorial amounts
shown below.
Attach a copy of this schedule to your paper return.
You can claim these amounts if you are eligible to claim the equivalent federal amounts on your federal Schedule 2.
If your spouse or common-law partner is filing a return, use the amounts that they entered on their Form NU428.
If your spouse or common-law partner is not filing a return, use the amounts they would enter on their Form NU428 as if they
were filing a return, and attach their information slips.
If your spouse or common-law partner was not a resident of Nunavut at the end of the year, special rules may apply.
For more information, call the Canada Revenue Agency at 1-866-426-1527.

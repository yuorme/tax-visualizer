Information
Eligibility
You can elect to stop making QPP contributions if you meet all the
following conditions:

•• You are at least 65 years of age but under 73 at the end of the year.

•• You are receiving a QPP or CPP retirement pension.

•• During the year, you did not revoke an election to stop making
QPP contributions.
You must wait until the day after you turn 65 before making the election
to stop making QPP contributions.
NOTE
It is generally advantageous to continue contributing to the QPP after the
age of 65. For more information, go to the Retraite Québec website
at retraitequebec.gouv.qc.ca/QPPcontributions.
Conditions for the election
You can complete this form only once a year per employer.
This means that if you elect during the year to stop making QPP
contributions but want to restart making contributions, you have to wait
until the following year to revoke your election. For example, if you elected
to stop making QPP contributions in June, you have to wait until at
least January 1 of the following year to revoke your election and restart
your QPP contributions.
Similarly, if, during the year, you revoke an election made in a previous year
and restart making QPP contributions, you have to wait until the following
year to file a new form to make another election to stop making
contributions. You cannot complete the form a second time to make an
election in the year.
Election
If you want to stop contributing to the QPP, you must complete this form and
give a copy to your employer along with a proof of age and a proof that you
are receiving a QPP or CPP retirement pension. If you work for more than
one employer, give each employer concerned a copy of this completed form.
If you change jobs and do not want to make QPP contributions, you must
complete a new form RR-50-V and give a copy to your new employer. Send
the completed forms to one of the following addresses:
Montréal, Laval, Laurentides, Lanaudière and Montérégie
Revenu Québec
C.P. 3000, succursale Place-Desjardins
Montréal (Québec) H5B 1A4
Québec City and other regions
Revenu Québec
3800, rue de Marly
Québec (Québec) G1X 4A5
We recommend that you keep a copy of the forms for your records.
When is your election effective
Your election is effective on the first day of the month after the date you
give a copy of this form to your employer. Your employer should stop
deducting QPP contributions from the first pay in the month following the
month they receive the copy of this form.
Your election will stay in effect until you revoke it or until December 31 of
the year you turn 72.
Revocation of an election
If you want to revoke an election made in a previous year and restart
making QPP contributions, you must complete this form and give a copy
to your employer. If you work for more than one employer, give each
employer concerned a copy of this completed form. Send the completed
forms to one of the addresses given above. We recommend that you keep
a copy of the forms for your records.
Your revocation is effective on the first day of the month after the date you
give a copy of this form to your employer. Your employer should start
deducting QPP contributions from the first pay in the month following the
month they receive the copy of this form.
Your revocation for a given employment will stay in effect until you elect
to stop making QPP contributions in a later year or until December 31 of
the year you turn 72.
